    How the numbers are built

    • Sarda Farms milk: 4 g protein per 100 ml as on your pack; fat about 3.5 g, so roughly 66 kcal per 100 ml. Calcium assumed 120 mg per 100 ml.
    • Epigamia Natural: 6.1 g protein, 55 kcal and 168 mg calcium per 85 g cup, scaled to your 10 g protein serving (about 140 g).
    • Protinex: 34 g protein, 373 kcal, 54 g carbs and about 1,300 mg added calcium per 100 g; one serving is modelled as 35 g.
    • Soya: 50 g dry Nutrela per bowl (52 g protein, 13 g fibre, 350 mg calcium, 20 mg iron per 100 g). Bread: a 5 g-protein slice of 100 kcal. Avocado: 150 g of flesh. Walnuts: 2 g per half.
    • Meals are an estimate for home-style lunch and dinner. Pick "Don't count meals" to see the routine on its own.
    • Leucine is estimated from each food's typical leucine share: dairy about 9.8%, whey 11%, soy 8%, wheat 7%.
    • Scores: Protein weighs daily total (75%) and how many meals cross the leucine trigger (25%). Nutrients averages coverage of fibre, omega-3s, iron, B12, potassium and magnesium. Safety starts at 100 and loses points for calcium above limits, saturated fat above 10% of calories, refined carbs and carnitine. Fuel score is 40% protein, 30% nutrients, 30% safety.
    • Calorie target uses Mifflin–St Jeor for a man, an activity factor, then −400 kcal to lose fat or +250 to build.

    Rules that move the needle

    +1 rep
    Double progression

    Work each lift in its rep range. Once you hit the top of the range on every set, add 2.5 kg and start again at the bottom.

    1–3 RIR
    Stop 1 to 3 reps short of failure

    Leave one to three reps in the tank on most sets. You only need to go to failure on the last set of isolation moves.

    8–10k
    Steps, every day

    Walking burns more on a cut than extra cardio, and it doesn't eat into recovery. Take a 10-minute walk after lunch and after dinner.

    7.5 h+
    Sleep protects the muscle

    In a dieting study, sleeping 5.5 hours instead of 8.5 cut fat loss by 55% and raised the share of weight lost as muscle.

    Wk 6
    Deload every 6th week

    Keep the weights but halve the sets. Your joints and nervous system catch up, and you come back stronger.

    Stop
    Pain is not a progress signal

    A sharp or joint pain means swap the exercise. Muscle soreness after 1 to 2 days is normal. Pain in the joint is not.

    Breathe

    Controlled slow breathing is one of the quickest ways to calm your nervous system. In a Stanford study, 5 minutes of cyclic sighing a day improved mood more than mindfulness meditation.

    Tonight's page

    Prompt

    Sleep window

    Wind-down, last 60 minutes

    Keep the room cool (around 19 to 21 °C with the fan on) and dark. Get morning light within an hour of waking. It sets your body clock more strongly than anything you do at night.

    Founder mode, without the burnout

    Running Naira means launches, suppliers and ad numbers on your mind at 1 a.m. These are the tools with the best evidence for that kind of load.

    Worry
    A 15-minute worry window

    Pick a fixed time, say 6 p.m. When a worry shows up outside it, jot it down and park it until then. Trials show this cuts worry and helps sleep.

    Work
    Shutdown ritual

    End work by writing tomorrow's top 3 and saying "done for today". An open to-do list keeps your mind chewing on it. Writing the plan down lets it stop.

    Focus
    90-minute deep blocks

    Do one block before noon with your phone in another room. Keep ads, DMs and supplier calls for the afternoon.

    Comparison
    Mute the scroll

    Scrolling other brands' Instagram at night is work that feels like rest. Batch your competitor research into one slot a week.

    People
    One real conversation a day

    Loneliness is a well-documented founder risk. Call or meet someone who isn't about the business. It protects you as much as sleep does.

    Emotion
    Name it to tame it

    Labelling a feeling ("anxious about the launch") lowers the brain's alarm response in imaging studies. The mood meter above does exactly this.
