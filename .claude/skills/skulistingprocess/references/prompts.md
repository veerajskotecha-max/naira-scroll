# Prompt systems

Copy these blocks verbatim. They are what makes a batch read as one shoot rather than a pile of renders.

## Ecom (plinth) prompt

Order: product reference FIRST, plinth set reference SECOND (`8cf120d5-4319-4aee-a284-5f0edc11a513`).

```
<product_desc>
IMAGE 2 = the set: a cream plaster display block on a warm ivory studio sweep. SET — COPY FROM IMAGE 2: same cream plaster block, same surface, same warm ivory palette, same soft diffused key from the upper left, same shadow softness, same camera feel.

Move the product from IMAGE 1 into the set from IMAGE 2.

PRODUCT — LOCKED, NOTHING HERE MAY CHANGE. It is a real photographed object being relocated, not redrawn. Keep the exact LENGTH, BREADTH, thickness and overall SIZE, and the proportion of every part to every other part. Keep the exact COLOUR — same metal tone, same polish, same stone colours in the same order. Keep the exact COUNT and POSITION of every stone, prong, bead and bezel.

STAGING: <per shot, see below>

REST AND CONTACT — THIS IS THE MOST IMPORTANT RULE. The piece physically RESTS on the plaster block. There is visible contact between the metal and the surface, and a soft grounded contact shadow directly beneath the point of contact that gets darker and tighter where they touch. Nothing floats, nothing hovers, nothing balances impossibly on an edge or a point, nothing is suspended in mid-air, no part of the piece is cut off by or sunk into the block. Gravity is obvious.

Real product photography for an ecommerce listing. Sharp on the product, shallow but honest depth of field, clean natural colour, no HDR, no CGI or 3D render look, no digital over-smoothing. Vertical 3:4, product filling roughly two thirds of the frame and centred. No text, no watermark, no logo, no graphics, no hands, no model.
```

### Staging by category

**Earrings**

- *hero* — HERO ANGLE — the main listing image. Both earrings on the flat top face of the plaster block: the left one standing upright, resting on its own lower edge with the front face square to the camera, the right one lying flat and slightly overlapping it, both fully in contact with the plaster. Camera at product height, slightly above.

- *angle* — THREE-QUARTER DETAIL. One earring only, resting tilted against the front edge of the block so the profile and the depth of the piece read — how far it stands off the ear, the thickness of the metal, the back of the setting. The second earring sits soft and out of focus behind it.

- *overhead* — OVERHEAD PLAN VIEW. Both earrings lying flat on the top face of the block, viewed straight down, arranged with a small even gap between them, their shadows short and directly beneath.


**Sets**

- *hero* — HERO ANGLE — the main listing image. Every piece in the set arranged together on the flat top face of the plaster block, all of them in contact with the plaster, composed so nothing overlaps confusingly and each piece is fully legible. Camera about 35 degrees above.

- *angle* — THREE-QUARTER ANGLE. The same set from product height, the larger piece behind resting against the block and the smaller piece in front of it, both fully supported.


**Bracelets**

- *hero* — HERO ANGLE — the main listing image. The bracelet lies on the flat top face of the plaster block in its natural relaxed open curve, the full length in contact with the plaster, clasp and extender visible at the front, no part hanging off the block. Camera about 35 degrees above.

- *drape* — DRAPED ANGLE. The bracelet drapes over the front top edge of the plaster block, part of it on the top face and part falling naturally down the front face, so the weight and flexibility of the chain read. Where it crosses the edge it stays in contact with the plaster the whole way.

- *macro* — CLASP AND LINK MACRO. Close study of the clasp and the two or three links either side of it, the bracelet still lying supported on the plaster, the rest of the length running soft out of frame.


**Pendants/Charms**

- *hero* — HERO ANGLE — the main listing image. The charm rests face-up on the flat top face of the plaster block, the chain running back in a loose relaxed S behind it and over the back edge. Camera about 35 degrees above, the charm centred and fully legible.

- *drape* — DRAPED ANGLE. The chain drapes over the top front corner of the block with the charm hanging just below the corner against the front face, in contact with the plaster.

- *macro* — CHARM MACRO. Close study of the charm filling the frame, resting on the plaster, the chain going soft either side.


**Necklaces**

- *hero* — HERO ANGLE — the main listing image. The necklace drapes over the top corner of the plaster block, the chain following the two faces of the block and staying in contact with the plaster the whole way, the pendant or the front section hanging at the front where the two faces meet. Camera at product height, slightly above.

- *flat* — FLAT COIL ANGLE. The necklace laid in a loose relaxed coil on the flat top face of the block, the whole chain in contact with the plaster, clasp visible, viewed from about 40 degrees above.

- *macro* — PENDANT MACRO. Close study of the pendant or the front feature of the chain, resting on the plaster, a short run of chain either side of it going soft, the block edge behind.


**Rings**

- *hero* — HERO ANGLE — this is the main listing image. The ring STANDS UPRIGHT ON THE FLAT TOP FACE of the plaster block, resting on the bottom arc of its shank, which sits flat and fully in contact with the plaster. Tilt the ring back about 35 degrees away from the camera so the lens looks slightly down into the circle of the band and the setting reads at the top. The FULL band is visible from top of setting to the point of contact — never cropped, never sunk into the block, never balanced on a corner or an edge. Camera at ring height, slightly above. A short soft contact shadow falls to the lower right.

- *side* — THREE-QUARTER LYING ANGLE. The ring LIES ON ITS SIDE, flat on the top face of the plaster block, the whole circle of the band in contact with the plaster. Camera about 40 degrees above, looking down across the ring so the band reads as a full open circle and the setting sits at the upper left of that circle, tilted toward the lens. A soft shadow spreads out beneath the whole band.

- *macro* — SETTING MACRO. Close study of the front of the ring — the stone or stones and the metal immediately around them fill the frame. The ring is still lying supported on the plaster, front edge of the block behind it, band running out of frame at the bottom. Very shallow focus on the front stone, the rest of the band falling soft.

## Worn prompt

Order: model reference FIRST, product reference SECOND. The crop rule is the whole trick.

### FRAMING
```
FRAMING — THE SINGLE MOST IMPORTANT RULE, DO NOT BREAK IT. This is a cropped luxury jewellery campaign shot, not a portrait. THE EYES ARE NOT IN FRAME. The top edge of the frame cuts across the face at or below the bottom of the nose, so only the lips, chin, jawline, neck, collarbone and shoulder are visible. Never show an eye at all. NO EYES AT ALL. Not one eye, not part of an eye, not an eyelash, not an eyebrow. If any part of an eye would appear, crop tighter. Never show the whole head. Never show the top of the head or the hairline above the brow. The jewellery, not the face, is the subject of the photograph.
```

### SKIN
```
SKIN — real and luminous, the way a high-end beauty campaign renders it. A natural sheen along the collarbone and the top of the shoulder. Visible fine pores at close range, a soft down of fine hair catching the light at the jaw and forearm, real subtle asymmetry, one or two small natural marks, faint natural colour variation across the cheek and the neck. Warm wheatish Indian skin tone. NOT plastic, NOT waxy, NOT airbrushed, no beauty filter, no digital smoothing, no uniform poreless surface.
```

### LIGHT AND SET
```
LIGHT AND SET — IDENTICAL IN EVERY IMAGE: a bright high-key studio set on a clean soft warm-white seamless background. One large soft key from the front left, a broad white bounce filling from the right so shadows stay open and gentle, and a discreet rim light behind catching the hair and the top of the shoulder. Bright, airy and luminous, never moody, no hard shadow anywhere.
```

### WARDROBE
```
WARDROBE AND STYLING — IDENTICAL IN EVERY IMAGE OF THIS SET, DO NOT VARY. She wears a fine-strap slip in warm marigold-ochre silk with a soft sheen — a saturated golden-amber, not beige, not cream, not yellow-green. The strap is narrow and sits on the shoulder; the neckline is simple and cut low enough that the collarbone and the base of the throat are completely clear. The silk catches the light with soft irregular folds and a real fabric weave. This colour is what the jewellery reads against — the warm ochre behind the metal is what separates it from the skin.
```

### HAIR
```
HAIR — this is where the picture becomes real, give it the most attention. Very dark brown, almost black, with warm chestnut depth where the light hits. Soft loose waves, not poker-straight and not curled. INDIVIDUAL STRANDS ARE SEPARATELY VISIBLE — the light picks out single hairs at the edge of the fall, a few fine flyaways stand away from the surface, and there is a bright rim of backlight running along the hair where it meets the background. Never a solid glossy helmet of hair, never a smooth painted mass, never a uniform block of black.
```

### CAMERA
```
CAMERA — 100mm macro at f/4, shot close. The jewellery is razor sharp and every stone, link, claw and bead is separately readable. Skin falls off gently behind it. Same warm neutral colour grade in every image. High-end commercial jewellery photography for an ecommerce listing, the quality of a Swarovski or Pandora campaign. Vertical 3:4. No text, no watermark, no logo, no graphics. Exactly one person. No jewellery other than the product described.
```

### Per category styling note
```
Earrings: hair swept back and tucked fully behind the ear on the product side, loose on the other.
Necklaces: hair pushed off both shoulders so the chain and collarbone are clear.
Bracelets: ochre silk strap and shoulder in frame behind the raised arm, nothing covering the wrist.
Rings: hand bare to the wrist, almond nails with sheer milky nude polish, exactly five fingers.
```
