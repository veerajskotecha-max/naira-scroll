# NAIRA PETITE — Exhibition stock log

One A4 page to count stock in and out at an exhibition. It lists every jewellery listing in the Petite Index (55),
each with its photograph, name, SKU, metal and price. Each row has write-in boxes for the storage box number,
the quantity at the start, the quantity at the end, and the number sold (start minus end).

- `naira-petite-exhibition-stock-log-A4.pdf`: print at 100% (A4, no bleed, white page for pen)
- `naira-petite-exhibition-stock-log-A4-preview.png`: preview

The header has lines for the exhibition, dates, venue, table or stall, and who counted at the start and at the end.
The foot has the totals and a sign-off. Prices are as listed on nairaflore.com on 2 October 2026. "Sold out" marks
pieces that were sold out on the website that day; log them if you have them in a box.
Rebuild with `build/build_log.py` (it reads the session scratchpad).
