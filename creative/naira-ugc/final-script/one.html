Why the offer lands here

Bolting BUY 2 GET 20% onto the end would undo the script. The whole ad is her admitting she doubted it — so the strongest possible proof is that she went back and bought more. That is revealed preference, not a claim, and it makes the ladder read as a consequence rather than a pitch.

It also serves the number: two pieces lands at ₹3,198, above the ₹1,800–2,400 AOV target. A bundle ladder is the one discount that raises order value instead of devaluing the piece.

Production — the ones that cost most

  • Motion in frame one, first cut inside 3s, product on the wrist at 0.0s.
  • Captions fixed at y 1150–1330. Nothing below y=1420 — Instagram’s chrome. 68–76px, weight 700–900, white, 3–5px dark stroke, no box.
  • Licensed music, ducked 12–16 dB under the voice, resolving on the end card. No trending audio — not cleared for paid.
  • Record the clasp click live — ten minutes, best audio in the reel.
  • Macros: tripod, tap to focus then hold to lock AE/AF, clean the piece each take. Plain wall, one white balance, face on the tripod.

Never say

  • “Waterproof” — the FAQ allows incidental contact only
  • “18K gold” — it is 18K gold-plated over steel, and this script says so out loud, which is its whole strength
  • A bare price with no delivery figure
  • “Only a few left” / “ends tonight” unless true on the day
  • “Link in bio” — it is a paid ad with a real button
  • Any competitor’s name
