07  /  THE HOOK ENGINEWhat the first three seconds have to do

Hooks are not decoration on the front of a script. On cold traffic they are most of the result, and they are the only element you can swap without reshooting anything.

Reels runs roughly 5–8 percentage points above Feed for the same asset, so benchmark by placement or you will kill a working creative. Note the caveat that matters most: hook rate is a kill signal, not a pick signal. No published research establishes a reliable relationship between thumbstop, hold rate, CTR and ROAS. Use it to stop paying for ads nobody watches — never to choose a winner. Your BRAND.md already has the right gates: hook <20% at 5k impressions → kill; outbound CTR <0.6% at 8k → kill; never kill before ~1,000 impressions or 48–72 hours.

The finding that should decide your hook mix

Negative beats positive on cold traffic, and it is not close

Across 52 DTC accounts, measured as CAC against a benefit-led baseline on cold prospecting:

The gap in the market, measured

Meta Ad Library, India, ACTIVE ads, 17 Sep 2026

Of roughly 1,500 active Indian jewellery ads surfaced: two objection-first headline hooks, one durability-mechanism hook. Everything else is a price, an offer or a product name. GIVA is running “Enjoy UP TO 50% OFF today” across ~415 ads. Palmonas is running “PALMONAS MEGA SALE” across ~141. Rubans — your closest competitor — runs a line literally titled “Demi Fine 18K Gold Plated Stainless steel” across ~188 ads, alongside price-in-headline carousels from ₹768 to ₹3,341.

The two best hooks in the entire pull came from brands you have never heard of: Iqaya Designs — “No Green Skin. Just Gold.” and Aurel.India — “Jewellery You Can Actually Wear Every Day”, where the word Actually is doing all the objection work. Four words each, category-specific, zero discount.

Also worth copying: Palmonas is running at least one ad through a creator’s own page (partnership / whitelisted). That is the single most copyable tactic in the pull and it is free reach you are not using with Riya.

A spoken objection-first hook carrying a physical proof act is close to unoccupied space in Indian jewellery right now. That is the lane, and it is the same conclusion your 498-creative BRAND.md audit reached by a different road: nobody in this category argues craft.

Hooks that are burnt — do not write these

POV: you found the perfect…Stop scrolling!Wait, you need to see thisThis changed my lifeTikTok made me buy itDay in my lifeAny open on product against white

Hooks fatigue faster than any other creative element. Assume a 2–4 week shelf life and refresh on cadence, not on performance collapse. Meta’s ranker also suppresses near-duplicates, so five variants of the same sentence are one ad in the auction, not five.

07b  /  THE HOOK BANKForty lines, written for this catalogue

Every line is a spoken first line under twelve words, with a matching 3–8 word overlay. Every number in them is real and checked against your live PDPs. None makes an outcome guarantee, names a competitor, uses false urgency, or addresses a personal attribute. Hinglish lines keep the spec in English, where authority lives, and the emotion in Hindi, where believability lives.

How to use this bank

Lines 01–21 carry the majority of cold spend — that is where both the CAC data and the category gap point. Lines 22–27 are your price-led cold set; run them without a discount badge. Lines 28–40 are warm, retargeting and festive.

Do not write forty scripts. Write one body and forty first lines. A voiceover reel lets you swap the opening three seconds without touching the creator, the picture or the grade — which is the single biggest budget efficiency available at ₹3,000/day. Four hooks against one body, everything else held constant, 5–7 days of data, then test CTAs against the winner.
