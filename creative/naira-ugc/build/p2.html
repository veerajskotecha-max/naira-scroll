Where the ceiling is

5.4 → 7.6

Re-edit only. No reshoot, no Riya, no new VO. Sixteen of the twenty craft defects are timeline work. Roughly four hours. See §10.

7.6 → 8.8

Re-edit + new VO on the same picture. Rewrite to the objection-first structure, re-record, re-cut to 26s. One day.

8.8 → 9.4

The VO reel. A purpose-shot proof reel — macro, water demo, scale reference, foley recorded live. Half a day of shooting. See §8.

What a 10 would require, honestly

Nothing in this format reaches ten on the first attempt, and you should be suspicious of anyone who says otherwise. The published winner rate for accounts under $10K/month is 3.8% — about 24 creatives tested before one lands. That number is already in your own BRAND.md. A “10” is not a video you make; it is the one out of twenty-four that the auction tells you is a 10. What this document can do is make sure all twenty-four are built on the right structure, so the hit rate is 3.8% of good ideas rather than 3.8% of guesses.
