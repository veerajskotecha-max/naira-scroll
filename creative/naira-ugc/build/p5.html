08  /  THE VOICEOVER REEL“Day Sixty” — the build

Why voiceover is the right format for Naira, specifically

A talking head sells credibility — the face is the proof, so the script has to be a claim about the speaker. A voiceover sells the product — the picture is the proof, so the script is a claim about the object and the voice narrates what the eye is already seeing. Four reasons that matters here:

  1. Your unclaimed lane is craft, and craft has to be seen. Nobody in this category argues setting, cut or finishing. You cannot argue it over a face at arm’s length. You argue it in macro.
  2. VO reels tolerate faster cutting — roughly a cut every 1.5–2.5s, because there is no lip-sync to respect. Talking head needs a b-roll insert every 4–6s just to avoid the mid-roll dead zone.
  3. You can swap the hook without the creator. Write one body and five first lines and one shoot becomes five ads. At ₹3,000/day this is the single biggest efficiency available to you.
  4. It removes the ceiling fan. No room, no background, no lighting continuity problem — one window, one surface, one hand.
The concept

Objection-first, proof-led, price-honest. Open on the objection every Indian buyer of ₹1,000 jewellery already holds — it will go green — then spend the middle of the reel physically disproving it, and close on a real number rather than a percentage. This is the hook family with the lowest measured CAC on cold traffic, in the one angle where you have physical proof and the category has only adjectives.

Non-negotiables before you shoot this

“Day sixty” must be true. Put a ring on someone on a dated day, photograph it that day, and shoot the reel sixty days later. If the number is invented the whole reel is a lie and the one angle you own becomes the one that gets you reported. The same applies to “two weddings” and to any review count you quote.

The water demo is a tap, not a pool. Your FAQ says incidental contact — rain, washing hands — and says remove before swimming. A tap take is fully supported and reads exactly as convincingly. Do not shoot a pool.

Sister cuts from the same rushes

15s compression

Hook + proof + price + CTA. Drop the problem beat and the spec beat. For Stories, for retargeting, and as the length test.

ASMR-lite, no VO

Box lid, tissue, clasp, chain pour, the tap. Text cards only. Sound-off safe by construction, and the cheapest variant to make.

Static / carousel sibling

For jewellery, statics out-performed video on CTR in a 12-month account teardown (~4%+ at ~$0.25 CPC vs Reels ~3.4%). Build one per concept.

Portfolio target is roughly 55–65% static, 35–45% video. One shoot → 5 hooks × 2 lengths + ASMR cut = 11 video assets, plus the statics. Tag every clip at ingest by action (“tap take”, “claw macro”, “coin scale”, “box open”) so anyone can assemble without re-watching rushes.
