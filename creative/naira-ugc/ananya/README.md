# Ananya — three reel ideas (Halo Curve Ring + Baroque Pearl Lariat)

`../NAIRA_Ananya_Three-Reel-Ideas.pdf` — 2 pages.

## The through-line

Both pieces are **irregular by design**, and that is already written into Naira's own product copy:

- Halo Curve Ring — *"Two stones that do not match, and that is the point."* Each stone sits in
  *"its own irregular bezel, shaped to follow the stone rather than correct it."*
- Baroque Pearl Lariat — baroque means the pearl is not round, so no two are the same shape.

In a category selling flawless, matched and symmetrical, **imperfect on purpose is an unclaimed
position** — and it is the craft argument the BRAND.md competitor audit said nobody makes.

## The three

| | Idea | Piece | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | **They don't match** | Ring | Cold traffic — reported speech + craft |
| 02 | **One necklace, three necklaces** | Lariat | Saves & styling — answers "what do I wear it with" |
| 03 | **Nothing here is pretending** | Both | The pairing offer — AOV ₹3,283 |

Idea 01's best asset is the tiger eye's **chatoyancy** — the sheen physically travels across the
stone as the hand moves. It is a filmable property no competitor can fake, because most don't use
a natural stone. **Confirm the tiger eye is genuinely natural before airing.**

Idea 03 answers the category's central anxiety (*is it real*) by saying exactly what each stone is.
A rival cannot copy that without rewriting their own product pages.

## Six discrepancies flagged on page 1

1. **The offer differs from the brief.** Briefed as buy 2 = 20% / buy 3 = 30%. Live site says
   **10% / 20%**. `../NAIRA_FINAL_Script_I-Almost-Didnt-Order-It.pdf` states 20/30 and is now wrong.
2. **Shipping is free**, not ₹150. Every script saying *"one-fifty delivered"* now overstates price
   — including the locked Sejal script.
3. **Lariat is ₹1,599**, not the ₹2,299 in the client's screenshot.
4. **The ring's meta description says "this solid gold band"** on an 18K-plated piece. It is in the
   social preview, so it is what gets shared. BIS hallmarking risk — fix on site before the ad runs.
5. **"Last 24 hours: 16 pieces sold"** on both PDPs — false-popularity under the CCPA dark-patterns
   guidelines unless live and true.
6. Material strings still say **waterproof** (contradicts the FAQ); the ring's says
   **brilliant-cut zircone** when it holds a tiger eye and a champagne crystal.
