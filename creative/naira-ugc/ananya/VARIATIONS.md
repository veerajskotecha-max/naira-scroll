# Ananya — three variations

`../NAIRA_Ananya_Three-Variations.pdf` — 5 pages, one script per page.
Source: `v.html` + `r2.css` (shared with `r3.html`).

## Why three structures, not three hooks

Swapping only the first line leaves three creatives that are near-identical underneath, and Meta's
ranker treats those as one — you pay for three and learn from one. These differ in **opening
device**, **lead product** and **register**:

| | Script | Opening device | Leads with | Register | Length | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | Two people have asked if this ring is broken | Reported speech | Ring | Sceptical | 35s | **Cold prospecting** |
| **B** | One necklace. Three necklaces. | **Silent demonstration** — no voice for 2.2s | Lariat | Practical | 25s | **Saves / styling** |
| **C** | Nobody gave me these | Declaration | Both | Warm | 25s | **Warm + festive** |

## A — "Two people have asked if this ring is broken" · 35s · 82 words

Objection-first review, refined from `r3.html`. The closer calls the hook back and flips it:
*"Necklace, easily. Ring only if you want people to ask."* Objection-first is the lowest-CAC
family on cold traffic.

## B — "One necklace. Three necklaces." · 25s · 54 words

**Opens on a picture, not a person.** Three hard cuts of the lariat at three lengths on three
necklines — it has to genuinely look like three separate necklaces — then the first words at 2.2s:
*"That's the same necklace."* The viewer has already been fooled, and that is the hook.

Answers the lariat's real blocker (*what do I wear it with*) three times before she speaks. Styling
is the save-heavy format, and saves sit second only to watch time as a ranking signal. It is also
the only one of the three that opens on an object, so it reads differently in the feed even
alongside the other two.

## C — "Nobody gave me these" · 25s · 58 words

Self-purchase rather than gift — *"I bought them for myself. On a Tuesday. For no reason."* Closes
on *"Stop waiting for someone else to buy you jewellery."* The right register for Karwa Chauth
through Diwali, when the whole category will be shouting about gifting.

**Do not run C and A in the same ad set** — C is warm, A is cold prospecting.

## Shared shot list

Eleven setups, one afternoon. Six of them feed two or three scripts. Page 5 maps every shot to the
variations that use it — **shoot the list, not the scripts**.

## Constraints, all three

- Every spec is an **on-screen chip**, never a line in her mouth.
- **No mineral is named.** The ring page still says tiger eye, brilliant-cut and "solid gold band";
  none of the three repeats any of it. The page needs fixing separately.
- Shell pearl, never "pearl". No "waterproof", "solid gold", "18K gold", "link in bio".
- **None mentions the pairing offer.** A review that pitches a bundle stops being a review, and the
  site says `BUY2` = 10% while the brief said 20%. Put the offer in the caption.

## Before she records

A says two people have *already asked*; C says she *bought* them. If the pieces were gifted, C needs
rewriting and A takes the alternate hook — *"I nearly sent this ring back"*, closing on *"Ring only
if you like things that argue."* **B is true either way.**
