# Ananya — three scripts (the simple set)

`s3.html` + `s3.css` → `../NAIRA_Ananya_Three-Scripts.pdf`, 4 pages.

Supersedes the A–F variation sets as the thing actually handed to the
creator. Same two SKUs, one shoot. Structure is the same in all three and
deliberately plain: **hook, six lines about the pieces, close.** No beat
grid, no timecodes, no rationale essays on the page she reads from.

| | Hook | Runtime | Words | Placement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Someone asked me where this is from. | 26s | 62 | warm, retargeting |
| 02 | Don't buy this ring. | 28s | 66 | cold |
| 03 | Online, everything looks the same size. | 27s | 64 | saves |

Page 4 is the reference sheet: both pieces with prices and specs, the ten
shots that cover all three scripts (~40 min, one sitting), and four rules
that apply to everything.

## 01 — "Someone asked me where this is from."

The hook the brand picked itself. It never says *buy* — somebody else
already wanted the piece and she is only answering. Requires the ring
**visible in frame one**, or the line has nothing to land on.

Close: *"So if someone asks you — now you know."*

## 02 — "Don't buy this ring."

Direct contradiction: 25–40% under benefit-led hooks on cold traffic across
the 52-account DTC set. Disqualifying people is the most expensive-looking
thing a brand can do, so it reads as confidence rather than selling.

**Must be played completely level — no wink, no smile.** The moment it reads
as a trick it becomes reverse psychology and the script dies.

Close (the flip): *"If you want one odd thing people ask about — this is it."*

## 03 — "Online, everything looks the same size."

Size is the question nobody in this category answers, and 42% of shoppers
judge it from the photo alone — also the biggest single cause of jewellery
returns. This is the one people save.

Two rules or it fails: the referent must be **in the same frame** as the
piece, and there must be **no cut** between the object and what it is
measured against. A cut destroys the proof.

Close: *"Now you know exactly what's arriving."*

## Shared rules

- **Verify 17mm and 15mm against the physical pieces before she records.**
  Both come off the product page, and that same page is wrong about the
  stones.
- **No percentage is spoken in any script.** Site says `BUY2` = 10%, the
  brief said 20%. It lives in the caption until that's settled.
- `#Ad` top third, frame one, ~a third of each runtime. Brand carries the
  liability, not the creator.
- Captions clear the bottom 40%; darken the footage behind the words, never
  a grey box.

## Never say

"Tiger eye" · "natural stone" · "real pearl" · "solid gold" or "18K gold"
(it is 18K gold-**tone plated** over surgical steel) · "waterproof" ·
"only a few left" · any percentage off.
