# NAIRA — brand + ad system brief (source of truth for this job)

## Who
Naira / Naira Petite / Naira Flore — Indian **demi-fine** jewellery, launching D2C (nairaflore.com).
Product: 18K-plated **316L stainless steel** + CZ/shell-pearl. The wedge: **non-tarnish, waterproof, worn every day**.
Price ladder: studs/dainty rings ₹899–1,499 · hoops/drops/statement rings ₹1,499–2,000 · chains/cuffs ₹1,499–2,200 ·
tennis/statement necklaces ₹2,000–2,900 · sets ₹2,200–3,299. Blended AOV target ₹1,800–2,400. Floor ₹899.
Catalogue ~51–66 styles, 5 units per SKU. Budget ₹3,000/day. Destination = **collection page, never PDP**.

## Identity
- Wordmark: high-contrast serif "NAIRA", a small carnation/flower replacing the stem of the I.
- Identity palette: Cream `#FFF8F5` · Sage `#99B4AF` · Coral `#FFBDA8`. Soft watercolour florals on packaging.
- Ad-system palette: Cream `#FFF8F5` on imagery · Marigold `#E8973A` eyebrow + rule · Ink `#1A1614` on pale grounds ·
  Sage `#99B4AF` reserved for the logo lockup only.

## Type system (non-negotiable)
- Display: **Velista 400**, 64–72px on 1080×1350. Sentence case, **never all-caps**. Balanced wrap, max 3 lines.
- Support: **Jost 300**, 24–28px, tracking .08em. Uppercase only for the eyebrow.
- Placement: always in the empty third the frame was composed to leave. **Left-aligned, ragged right.**
  Never centred, never over the product, never over the scale referent.
- Contrast ≥4.5:1 against the pixels actually behind the type. If it fails, **darken that background region — never add a scrim box.**
- Logo bottom-left, 5% margin, single colour, ≤8% of canvas width.
- Bottom clearance **40%** keeps every template safe (Reels/Stories 35%, 40% with a disclaimer).
- Copy length: Reels allows **44 characters** of primary text — that is the binding constraint on the display line.

## Position (from 498 live competitor creatives, 6 Indian brands)
- **Nobody in this category argues craft.** No brand uses setting, cut, plating thickness or finishing as a theme.
  Anti-tarnish is the closest anyone gets (Palmonas 12%, Rubans 6%). The craft vocabulary is unclaimed → that is the lane.
- Word ownership: GIVA=silver 86% (+ only one saying hypoallergenic), CaratLane=diamond 71%, Tyaani=bridal 42%,
  Voylla=rakhi 75%. Palmonas and Rubans own nothing — discount-led accounts drift.
- **Discounting is not the entry fee.** Tyaani carries an offer in 0% of copy and holds the best median impression rank
  of the six plus the single highest-ranked creative of all 498. GIVA discounts in 80% and ranks second worst.
- Price fight unwinnable: Palmonas anchors ₹699, GIVA ₹999, Naira floor ₹899 — inside a ₹300 war with none of their volume.
  22,267 live "jewellery 50% off" ads in India vs **228** for "demi-fine gift for her".
- **So: Tyaani's refusal to discount + Rubans' willingness to show hard evidence — but the evidence is craft, not order counts.**

## The six creative territories (each dimension-verified)
1. **The craft proof** — claws visibly gripping the girdle, tool marks left in. "Four claws. Not glue."
2. **The demonstration** — prove it, don't narrate it. Waterline across a chain. "Shower in it. Swim in it."
3. **Actual size** — a known referent in frame. 42% of shoppers judge size from the image alone. "Actual size. No surprises."
4. **On Indian skin** — gold against warm brown skin; the asset a competitor sourcing the same factory cannot copy.
5. **The clasp as design** — hardware nobody advertises; name mechanisms as separate parts.
6. **The box as product** — in gifting, packaging sells as hard as the piece. Dual-read gift / self-purchase.

## Image-generation laws (hard-won, 400 generations audited)
1. **ONE product per frame. Never two.** Slot 1 = the product.
2. **Describe the product in ≤6 words.** Every adjective is a licence to re-imagine it.
3. **Two light sources — one large soft key, one small hard specular kicker.** Without the kicker gold renders as matte plastic.
4. **Scale must be built, not assumed** — restore all four channels explicitly:
   - Referent (a known object beside the piece, stating the ratio)
   - Optics ("honest macro optics, depth of field only a few millimetres deep")
   - Contact ("a dark believable contact shadow directly beneath where it touches")
   - Grain of matter (real fibre, real dust, believable imperfection)
   - Killer move: **substrate deformation** — the piece must visibly *do something* to what it rests on.
5. **Brief from the listing, not the product name.** "Solitaire Whisper Studs" is neither a solitaire nor gold.
6. **Name mechanisms as separate parts.** "Toggle clasp" renders as a round push clasp; "a straight bar passed through
   an open ring, both parts clearly visible" renders correctly.
7. **The reference governs structure; an explicit colour word overrides it.** Never state metal colour unless you looked.
8. Two-reference system: slot 1 product, slot 2 an empty setting plate — keep the plate's principal referent within
   ~2.5× of the product's largest dimension, or scale compresses toward the plate's referent.
9. Never: orange/brassy gold (first tell of cheap AI jewellery), invented halos/pavé, substituted cuts, a second piece.

## Operating gates
- Winner rate under $10K/mo = **3.8%** → expect ~24 creatives tested before one winner lands.
- Median Meta ad lifespan 4.0 days; effective creative lifespan 2–4 weeks.
- Thumbstop ≥22–25% · hold-to-15s ≥20% · link CTR 1.2–1.5%.
- Kill gates in order: hook <20% @5k impr → kill; outbound CTR <0.6% @8k → kill; ATC <3% @300 LPV → offer/page problem;
  zero purchases at 3× target CPA → kill. Never kill before ~1,000 impressions / 48–72h.
- **No fabricated scarcity, ever** (India dark-patterns guidelines name false urgency + false popularity).
  Real scarcity at 5 units/SKU is a genuine asset, but it must be real. Every millimetre figure must match the listing.
