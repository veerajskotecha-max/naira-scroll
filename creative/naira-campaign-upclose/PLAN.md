# NAIRA Petite · Up Close

Final campaign plan, written 1 Oct 2026, for 2 Oct to 11 Nov 2026. It holds 26 briefs (C01 to C26) to build; the best 20 builds ship. Campaign slug `upclose-oct26`. The machine-readable version is `plan/plan.json`.

> One Sales campaign, one idea: in the loudest month of the year, build every ad for a few inches away.

## 1. The idea

**Up close.** This October the Indian jewellery feed is built to be read from across the room: 40–80% off, codes, sparkle, emoji. NAIRA builds for the opposite distance.

Every ad is one in-stock piece (or one declared pair) and one true thing you only notice from a few inches away: the king fell and the hoops stayed, the rail left plain on purpose, a dome you can see light through, three colours instead of seven, a name that rearranges into A RAIN, nine hearts for nine nights. Each ad says it in one Velista line and/or one lowercase Cormorant turn. The piece is named at its live price with its exact material line in a quiet Jost caption, and the primary text tells that piece's own story.

Three chapters share that grammar:

- **The finish, seen up close.** This is the account's one clear win, and it carries the evergreen wave.
- **The house that sews.** NAIRA is a couture house first, so the spool, the swatch, the care label and the pattern key hold the jewellery.
- **The festive chapter.** Here the true detail meets the date: nine hearts for Navratri, two stones that don't match for Karwa Chauth, a prism that brings its own light for Diwali, two hearts that make a bow for Bhai Dooj.

Permanence is never shouted on the image. It is proved as a finish that survives a close look. The founder's **made to stay gold.** closes the primary text of every gold-coated or two-tone piece. Prism Rivière, the only rhodium-only hero, closes on *NAIRA Petite.* until the founder approves a rhodium variant, so no ad implies gold on a silver-tone piece.

## 2. Why it wins

1. It is the judges' consensus, built the way they asked. Two of the three judges scored the craft lens's 'Up Close' first (47.7 vs 46.4 and 46.2; 46 vs 45.5 and 41), and the third scored it a close second (7.9 vs 8.0) while asking for its best briefs and its stock guard. All three asked for the performance lens's media chassis, and two asked for the festive lens's dated frames. This plan is that combination: the craft idea and its product-truth discipline, the performance structure, and the festive frames that survive the never-list.

2. It rests on the account's clearest result, not on taste. In the same ad set 'finished to be seen up close' beat 'daily wear' 3.8 to 2.2 add-to-carts per ₹1,000 (p≈0.002) and 10 orders to 2. The only static with repeat orders was one piece, one tactile metaphor and a paradox line, with the piece's own story in the copy (49 add-to-carts at ₹115, ROAS 1.62); the same image with edit-and-price copy got 3. All 7 orders won by single-product statics bought the pictured piece, and 6 of them were prepaid. So every brief is one piece or a declared pair, one up-close truth and that piece's story, and no image carries an offer, a code or a percentage.

3. It takes the one position the season leaves open. Offer tiles are 22% of category ads, discounts run 40–80%, 'artisan' appears in 0 live Indian ads, nobody runs macro, and 1 of 24 tracked brands mentions Karwa Chauth. Quiet, detailed and owned is the loudest position available, and BUY2's 10% could never win the discount race anyway.

4. It puts the money where the stock and the proof are. 15 of the 26 briefs feature one of the six deep-stock pieces (Toggle Link Chain 80 units, Woven Gold Hoops 77, Prism Rivière 76, Heartbead 29, Brushed Gold Huggies 16, Baroque Shell 13; Shopify, 1 Oct), and 13 feature the five pieces that have already sold through ads. Every other piece has at least 3 units, the 1–2-unit SKUs are out, and a stock guard pauses any ad when its piece reaches 1.

5. It builds the basket without discounting the picture: three declared pairs (₹2,598, ₹3,598 and ₹3,698; the two larger ones clear the ₹3,000 free-shipping line even after BUY2) and BUY2 in every primary text, against 1.33 items per order and only 6 of 43 orders over ₹3,000.

6. It gives Meta's Andromeda 26 genuinely different candidates: 26 layout architectures across about a dozen visual worlds (plaster, chess, spool, cardamom, folded paper, prism light, silk and linen on model, packshots in drawn frames), each with a separately composed 9:16 for Stories, the cheapest add-to-cart placement and the source of all three Woven orders.

7. It fixes the money leaks the data shows: one campaign instead of two overlapping ones, Feed, Stories and FB Feed only, no cost cap, verdicts on Shopify-verified orders and add-to-carts per ₹1,000 (never CTR), a stop-loss, and the funnel fixes that cap any creative. Honest limit: creative alone will not close a 3.2x gap (₹3,320 per order now against ₹1,047 break-even). The realistic target is a blended ₹1,600 per order by 27 Oct, and break-even on the concepts that clearly win.

## 5. KPIs and scale/kill rules

| KPI | Target | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Shopify-verified cost per ad-won order | Break-even ₹1,047 (about ₹830 from 25 Oct); interim blended ₹1,600 by 27 Oct | ₹3,320 now; creative alone will not close a 3.2x gap |
| Add-to-carts per ₹1,000 | 3 or more | Account 3.3, Prism winner 8.7; read only after the duplicate sender is removed |
| Basket | AOV ≥ ₹2,600; ≥ 1.5 items per order; ≥ 25% of orders over ₹3,000; BUY2 on ≥ 20% of orders | Now ₹2,198, 1.33 items, 6 of 43 orders over ₹3,000 |
| Prepaid share | 60% or more | 42% of orders are COD now |
| Diagnostics | Share of orders containing the pictured piece; saves and shares per 1,000 impressions by concept; CTR; CPM | CTR is never a verdict: the higher-CTR variants lost on carts and orders |

- **Verdict timing.** An ad is judged at about ₹3,500 spent, or at day 7 if it has spent at least ₹1,500 by then. Under ₹700 by day 7 means Meta starved it: rotate it out unjudged.
- **Pass.** At least 3 add-to-carts per ₹1,000 and at least 1 verified order.
- **Kill.** Under 3 per ₹1,000 and no order at ₹3,500, or no add-to-cart at ₹2,000.
- **Hold.** Anything in between runs one more week.
- **Dated ads.** An early read at ₹1,500 or day 4 pauses any with zero add-to-carts. The rest run to the end of their window and are judged on it.
- **Scale a concept, not an ad.** At 3 or more verified orders at ₹1,300 or less each, re-cut it weekly, with a new crop or the same line on another deep-stock piece (the Missoma and Monica Vinader model). Keep the original running, and raise AS1 by 20% every 72 hours while blended cost per order stays at or under ₹1,300.
- **Frequency.** Refresh creative above a 7-day frequency of 2.5 in AS1 or 4 in AS2.
- **Stock guard.** Check Shopify every Monday. Pause a single-piece ad when its piece reaches 1 unit, and a pair ad when either piece does. Never write scarcity.
- **Stop-loss.** If ₹40,000 is spent and blended verified cost per order is above ₹2,500: cut AS1 to ₹1,000 a day, switch AS2 off, keep only passing ads, and fix the funnel before spending more.
- **Uncertainty.** Every verdict states its Poisson interval. Nothing on fewer than about 20 orders is established.

## 8. What came from where, and what the judges fixed

The craft lens won two of the three judges (47.7 and 46 against the performance lens's 46.2 and 45.5) and was a close second with the third (7.9 against 8.0). All three asked for the performance lens's media chassis, and two asked for the festive lens's dated frames. Of the 20 briefs the judges ranked best, the ones picked by all three are all here: THE KING FELL., A RAIN. (as craft C01 or performance P02), SHOWN LARGER., ONE LEFT PLAIN., EMERALD GREEN, not emerald., WORN, not saved., NO TWO ALIKE. and NINE NIGHTS.

Violations fixed:

- **False clasp copy.** 'A TURN, not a catch' (P08) is dropped. Every Toggle ad (C05, C13, C21) describes the spring-ring clasp and pavé-set gate that actually ship, and the product page gets corrected.
- **Banned image text in a live ad.** Ad 104 is paused at launch instead of running as a benchmark.
- **1–2-unit SKUs.** Solitaire Whisper Studs, Baroque Pearl Lariat, Bold Nocturne Bracelet, Verdant Eternity Band, Rose Verdant Band and Charm Box Chain are all out (P13, P16, P28, F15, F24, F28). Every piece in the pool has at least 3 units, and the stock guard pauses ads at 1.
- **Copy that contradicts the audit.** It is rewritten from the audited product truth: Chevron's three rails (C10), the Granule openwork (C11), the Ribbon Bead's two hearts (C22), the Baroque Shell's eight pearls plus a drop (C08). The unverified 'WOVEN, not cast.' and the Star Point and Ribbon Bow PDP lines are not used.
