## Read this first: five facts that change how the feed is built

1. **Prices are ₹799 to ₹2,599, not ₹24,000 to ₹44,000.** Every artifact, and the bag example in CLAUDE.md
   (2 pieces = ₹4,298), agrees. The CLAUDE.md line "pieces at ₹24,000–44,000" is wrong or stale. The Edition One
   price ladder is T1 plain metal ₹1,399–1,649 up to T4 statement ₹2,449–2,599, with endings 49 or 99.
   Prices also moved between September and October (Prism Rivière ₹1,699 on 7 Sep, then ₹2,399; Woven Gold Hoops
   ₹1,799, then ₹1,499; Ribbon Bow ₹2,299, then ₹1,599). **Re-check every price live before it goes on a post.**
2. **The owner's own ad account says "crazy beats plain".** Every jewellery ad to 12 Oct 2026: 108 ads, ₹1.59 lakh.
   Concept statics returned 1.44× ROAS against 0.57× for plain statics. Concept won outbound CTR in 10 of 10
   side-by-side ad sets. 4:5 Feed returned 1.79× and Stories 1.36×. The caveat: 10 of 16 concept sales came from
   one image ("Almost edible."), and Prism Rivière carried 12 of 16 concept sales at 1.63×. What won: a visual pun
   with the piece as the punchline, food and measuring tools, light pastel grounds, 4:5 Feed, pieces over ₹2,000.
3. **The proven hook formula** (Edition Two brief): an Indian culture rhyme at the piece's own scale, then a
   lowercase "X, not Y" correction that states the true material. Best ad: **"Almost edible." / "cubic zirconia,
   not candy."** This is the native bridge to the Indian-pop maximalist reference.
4. **Generated jewellery beside a prop comes out about 1.5× too big.** 34 of the 73 failed Contact Sheet frames
   failed on scale. The two highest-scoring frames in that round (9.5) were **composites**: the real piece cut
   from its own listing photo and set into a generated scene at true scale. For an illustrated feed, composite
   real product cutouts into drawn or generated worlds; do not ask a model to redraw the piece.
5. **Truth-in-copy rules are strict and owner-enforced.** Write "18k gold tone" or "silver tone", never "gold"
   alone, "solid", "real", "pure". Stones are "cubic zirconia", never "diamond"; Ruby, Emerald, Amethyst are colour
   names ("ruby-red cubic zirconia"). Never call green stones "emerald"; the house word is **Verdant**. Edition Two
   copy may make **no waterproof claims**. Edition One steel pieces may say waterproof only where the listing's
   Care block does (46 of 50 SKUs in September; Baroque Shell Bracelet, Pearl Legacy Necklace and Verdant Drop
   Earrings may not). No luxury look-alikes (Van Cleef clover/Alhambra, Hermès H, Cartier nail or love).
