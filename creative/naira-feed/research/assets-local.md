# Naira Flore: local and site imagery inventory

Swept 25 Oct 2026. 410 files, every one viewed on a labelled 4x4 contact sheet (`research/contact-local/sheet_00.jpg` to `sheet_25.jpg`; the record `id` NN.CC is sheet NN, cell CC). Full records with size, subject, shot type, palette, mood, quality, baked text, cut-out suitability, best use, source and stock: `research/assets-local.json`. Files: `assets/local/<source-folder>/`, original resolution.

Stock figures are `totalInventory` from the Shopify product read already in `research/raw/products_p*.json` (this sweep). Several of the most beautiful frames show pieces that are now at 0 to 5 units; they are flagged.

## What is here, in one paragraph

The jewellery photography that matters lives in three places. (1) The **catalogue PDFs**, from which I extracted the embedded Higgsfield camp photographs: four worlds (Apricot silk + fruit, Lilac paper + lavender, Sage celadon + leaf, and Vol. One's Long Afternoon / Steel & Gold / Red Room), 80 frames, the only local copies of these plates. Heroes and covers are 1417 to 1652 px wide; the tall 'c3' strips are only 685 px wide and the 'c2' macros about 670 px. (2) The site's **gilded/** set (31 styled frames of real pieces that look AI-generated: plinths, green velvet, worn crops, 928 to 1280 px). (3) **Customer photos** (real/ and ugc/, pink-box unboxings and worn shots). Everything else is either finished creative with baked type (21 paid-social ads from Lilac Hour, Soft Matter and three brand posters, 20 Up Close posts + 20 stories, Gilded Hour banners), Naira Flore couture imagery (outfits, not jewellery), concept renders of pieces that are not on sale, or brand graphics.
