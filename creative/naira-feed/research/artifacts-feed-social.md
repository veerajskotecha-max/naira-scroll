## 1. Captions for the twelve-post grid (Claude Doc, 16 Oct 2026)
URL: https://claude.ai/artifact/3xxucSaM6Evu9Jx3s2YLsB. Doc title: "Naira · Captions for the twelve-post grid".

**Purpose.** Captions for a 12-tile illustrated grid. The tile names match the "Sugarplay and The Red Room" artifact, which another agent is covering. The method:
- One joke per caption, told with a straight face. The tile is the setup and the caption is the second beat.
- Every joke comes from an Indian childhood detail that no demi-fine brand is using yet.

**Rules it records (the owner's caption system, "your caption system").**
- Structure: a hook under 125 characters, so it lands before "more". Then one or two short lines. Then a bracket kicker in the form `[ piece · material · wink ]`.
- Tags go in the first comment, five at most: #NairaFlore #DemiFineJewellery #DiwaliGifting, plus two per piece type.
- Banned: em-dashes, exclamation marks, emoji and urgency. No claim beyond "18k gold-coated", or "rhodium-plated" on silver-toned pieces.
- A "countdown rule": odd numbers are spelled out.
- Standard hyphens only, so Instagram search reads "demi-fine" and "gold-coated" as keywords.
- Post in reverse order, tile 12 first and tile 01 last.
- Suggested dates: every other day from 17 Oct, so that tile 10 lands before Karwa Chauth (29 Oct), Sweet Delivery on Dhanteras (6 Nov) and Sugar Play on Diwali (8 Nov).

**Influences it names.**
- The Economist's red posters: few words, and the reader finishes the joke.
- Oatly: honesty is funnier than hype.
- Sandy Liang: nostalgia is the product.
- Amul: gentle and Indian. Aunties and mothers are loved, never mocked.
- The category is shouting: Jaamni runs "Buy 1 Get 2 Free" and Soboraa runs "Festive Sale: Buy 2 Get 1 Free". Wit with restraint is open ground.
- Left out deliberately: "jewellery that speaks volumes" style lines, and the soan papdi regift joke as a lead, because brands have run it every Diwali since 2019 (FilterCopy, Swiggy, Zomato, Paisabazaar).
