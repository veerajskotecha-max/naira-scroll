Voice "warm, quiet, slow-made"; palette blush / cream / sage; tagline **"Softly, slowly, worn."** Floral is the house signature (the logo's I is a flower). Agency SOP: "calm and Gen Z-subtle tone", minimal transitions, colour-accurate, sober music.

4. **Dates that matter now** (from the caption doc's sources): Karwa Chauth Thu 29 Oct 2026, Dhanteras 6 Nov, Diwali 8 Nov; December = wedding season.
5. **Claim limits**: pieces are sourced from three Chinese suppliers (Yiwu J&D, KAVNAR, Foshan Yiss Fera); 18k gold-plated stainless steel + CZ + imitation/shell pearl. The couture side is genuinely hand-embroidered in India; the jewellery is not. Do not borrow the couture "made by artisan hands / karigar" language for Petite.

## Caption system already approved by the owner (from the 12-tile caption doc, 16 Oct)
- One joke per caption, told straight. The tile is the setup, the caption the second beat. Every joke from an Indian childhood detail no demi-fine brand uses yet.
- Hook under 125 characters (lands before "more"). Then one or two short lines, then a **bracket kicker: `[ piece · material · wink ]`**, e.g. `[ demi-fine earrings · 18k gold-coated · best of three ]`.
- No em-dashes, no exclamation marks, no emoji, no urgency. Tags in the first comment, five at most (#NairaFlore #DemiFineJewellery #DiwaliGifting + two per piece).
- Influences named: The Economist red posters (few words, reader finishes the joke), Oatly (honesty is funnier than hype: "They are. Real earrings."), Sandy Liang (nostalgia is the product), Amul (gentle, Indian, never mean; aunties and mothers loved, never mocked).
- Explicitly avoided: "jewellery that speaks volumes"-type lines; the soan papdi regift joke as a lead (used by brands every Diwali since 2019).
- Lines judged strongest: "Not locker gold. Tuesday gold." (10/10, "worth testing as an ad headline and a hang-tag line"); the paper fortune teller caption ("an aunty asks if they're real. They are. Real earrings."); the "second toffee" bracelet; the dabba rule ("the dabba never goes back empty").

## Owner notes and standing rules gathered
- Masala/spice-tin imagery vetoed.
- Never sand, tan, beige or golden backgrounds in generated images; gold must read warm-neutral, not brassy (Drive CLAUDE.md law 5). The current Shopify plinth backdrop is peach-cream: fine for PDP, but the brief for campaign worlds was colour, not beige.
- Always verify the piece in a generated image against the real product (two mislabels happened: YF8156, YF8439; and the Pale Hours proof sheet shows a ring under "Solitaire Whisper Studs").
- On-model: no face, one gesture (fingertip, ear, wrist, collarbone, nape).
- One outfit/piece = max 3 content pieces (agency SOP).
- Site rule: "18K gold tone & rhodium plated" are the listings' words; "18K gold" alone would claim gold content.
- Couture offers once ran "Flat 20% off on your first order"; jewellery now runs NAIRA10 and BUY2/BUY3.
