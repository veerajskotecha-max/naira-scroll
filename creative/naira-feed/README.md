The Instagram plan built from every image in the Higgsfield account: 4,676 completed generations, all reviewed
and given a role. Live page: https://claude.ai/artifact/PWo9GiaUAwNRpkKNdwQDsQ

| File | What it is |
|---|---|

## The idea

One row, one product, three ways. Each row of three is one family seen as the object (still life or plinth),
worn (on a person, for scale) and the detail (macro, for the setting). Six colour chapters of two weeks: The Red Room
(Diwali), The Index (cream, the catalogue), Sage, The Gift (Christmas and New Year), Lilac, Apricot. Fifteen posts
a chapter: seven singles, four carousels, four reels. Posting runs bottom-right to top-left so the finished grid
reads as designed. Reels at 12:30 IST, posts at 19:30 IST, three stories a day. A pinned evergreen row on top.

## Numbers

772 images placed: 93 covers, 122 carousel slides, 321 reel frames, 252 story frames, 8 highlight covers.
The rest are sorted: 1,547 in the ads bank (finished 4:5 and 9:16 frames), 194 catalogue packshots, 80 variants,
894 held for next season, 1,189 excluded with a reason each (background plates, clothing shoots, smeared
outpaints, processing steps, composite grids, rendered text, three art-director vetoes).
