# NAIRA NIWAS · one building, one window a post

The season told as one blush Bombay-Deco co-op building, three bays wide and four storeys tall, the exact shape of the 3 x 4 profile grid: every post is a reel or a moving carousel that finds its window, opens it and lights it on one true moment of present-day housing-society life with one real Naira piece inside, and the twelve covers, posted in order, assemble on the profile into the building itself, lit from the shops at street level to a wedding on the terrace.

Post natively from the Instagram app, strictly P1 to P12 (P12 ends top-left). Each folder holds the files to post in order (1080x1440, 3:4), a `4x5/` copy (1080x1350) for schedulers that only take 4:5, and `caption.txt` with the caption, the first comment and alt text. Re-check every price in Shopify the day before posting.

Every post moves. A reel folder holds `01.mp4` and its designed 1080x1920 `cover.jpg` (upload it as the reel's cover; the grid shows its middle 3:4). A carousel folder holds its slides in order, videos as `.mp4`; its grid tile is frame 0 of slide 1, and `cover.jpg` is that same frame as a still: if the app shows a different frame on the grid, post `cover.jpg` as slide 1 and the loop as slide 2.
