For the listing agent

  • Create every product as DRAFT, vendor Naira Petite, currency INR.
  • Options exactly as listed (Metal, Stone, Size, Month). SKU, price and inventory per variant from the table.
  • Add images in the numbered order via each image's public URL. Use the alt text given, and attach each variant's image to that variant.
  • Title, tagline and body copy are final. Put the size line under “Size & fit”.
  • Read the whole kit as JSON from listing.json or the Copy JSON button.

Do not

  • Do not publish anything that still has a “Confirm” note. The owner clears those first.
  • Do not create products for reorders (YF5244, YF7085, YF5144 necklace and bracelet, YF3925, YF8453, YF8156, YF5214 gold): they are already live. See “Live listings to update”.
  • Do not change any live product without the owner's go-ahead.

Pricing logic

  • Priced against the live Naira Petite range (₹799 to ₹2,749) and the PI unit cost: 3.7 to 6 times landed cost (PI price × ₹83 plus about 20% freight and duty), rounded to the store's x99 steps; set pieces match their live partners (Bold Nocturne Drops at the Bold Nocturne Bracelet's ₹1,599).
  • Colour and metal variants share one price, except the silver coil bangle (cheaper on the PI).
