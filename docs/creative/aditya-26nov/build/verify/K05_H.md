## Lock, clause by clause
- A pair of studs, identical: yes. Two earrings, the same design and size.
- Small smooth puffed heart, plain polished gold: yes. Domed and smooth, with no stones, engraving or outline. The reflections on it even match the supplier photo, including the dark window reflection in the centre and the small notch at the top cleft of the right heart.
- Heart upright, on a stud post: yes. The heart points down, lobes at the top. The post is hidden behind the heart, as it is in the supplier photo.
- A tiny gold loop from the heart's lower point: yes. There is a short vertical loop under the point on each earring.
- ONE small round gold jump ring: yes, exactly one on each earring.
- ONE round brilliant-cut clear stone: yes, one on each earring. It is round, not a pear, and clear with brilliant facets.
- FOUR fine gold claws at top, bottom, left and right: yes. The top claw is the small bail that goes through the jump ring, the same as in the supplier detail photo. The left, right and bottom claws have ball tips.
- The stone hangs free below the heart: yes. There is a clear gap of about 120 px between the heart's point and the stone, bridged only by the loop and jump ring.
- Proportions: stone width including claws compared with heart width is 0.81 in the frame and 0.85 in the supplier photo. Heart width compared with height is about 1.2 in the frame and 1.15 in both the supplier photo and the spec. Whole length compared with heart width is 2.11 in the frame and 2.30 in the supplier photo, so the hanging part is about 8% shorter relative to the heart. A shopper would not notice this.

- The stone hangs on a jump ring and is never fixed straight to the heart: holds.
- No chain: holds.
- No third earring: holds. There are exactly two.
