The 43 dropped fail that test in one of three ways:

1. **Pantry and kitchen food (31 shots):** grains, pulses, spices, savoury snacks, syrups and broken sweets: rice,
   sabudana, chana dal, rajma, mustard seeds, cloves, haldi, rock salt, curry leaves, hara chana, almonds, cashews,
   pistachio, makhana, chakli, khari, bhakarwadi, pani puri, petha, gur, chikki, soan papdi, rava laddoo, motichoor,
   kaju katli, gond, chashni, honey, star fruit, wheat, nimbu-mirchi. Jewellery does not belong in the kitchen; next to
   pantry food it reads cheap, and broken or crumbed food reads messy.
2. **Random objects linked only by a pun (8 shots):** a harmonium, a sewing spool, a skipping rope, a cotton-wool cloud,
   cowries from a dice game, a banana leaf, a money-plant cutting, a sequinned net scrap. The viewer cannot see why they
   are together without reading the line.
3. **Flowers with the wrong job (4 shots):** a marigold puja garland, wet rose petals in rose water, a gajra so big it
   swamped the necklace, a paranda that read as a blonde hair braid.

## The rule for round three

Every prop must answer "why is this here?" in one second, because it is:

- **where the piece is worn or given:** Diwali, a wedding or sangeet, a night out, gifting, getting ready, the season;
- **a true look-alike of the piece's shape, name or material**; or
- **a flower or whole fruit in the stones' colour.**

No pantry food, no spices or powders, nothing broken or crumbed, no object whose only link is a pun, no religious objects.
Same house style as before: one prop, a pale colour-coded ground, real light, the piece sharp and at true size; the Story
is text-free and the Feed carries the wordmark, one line and the name and price in the shot's ink.
