# Naira crazy ads: 20 concepts (22 Oct 2026)

The founder found the five-format set too basic. These 20 push the visual pun much further. Each one is a surreal or
deeply Indian joke that makes a stranger stop scrolling, then look twice at the jewellery. The account's best seller works
the same way: "Almost edible.", the Prism bracelet in a bowl of gummy sweets, 10 sales.

Rules that do not change:
- The jewellery is exact: construction, colour, metal, stone count and true size, from the product's lock and reference.
- The joke lives in the world around the piece, never in the piece itself.
- One product per frame.
- 9:16, one single continuous photograph, never a collage.
- The jewellery and the joke sit in the middle of the frame, with calm space for a headline.
- No text, logos, faces (nothing above the jawline) or extra jewellery.
- No real brands: no IRCTC logo, no Bajaj or Chetak badge, no Cadbury.

Every scene is staged and plausible, a real set a photographer could build, but it goes further than a still life: bold
colour, theatre, a story in one glance. Light is editorial: one strong key light, a hard specular kick on the metal, deep
colour.

## Results

Every frame was scored against the product's lock and reference image; 8 passes, and two frames at 7.5 run as reserves
(after the rest of their ad set). The scores, prompts and job IDs are in `build/shots_crazy_g*.json`.

| Result | Concepts |
|---|---|
| Passed (8 or more) | C01–C06, C08–C14, C16, C18–C20 |
| Reserve (7.5) | C07 (about 58 stones show in the ice where a 36 cm choker has about 110, so it reads short), C15 (each stud reads about 20 mm beside the star anise, against a true 15 mm) |
| Empty | C17, C21 |
