# Naira colour-coded ads: 50 shots, one per product (23 Oct 2026)

The founder's direction: elegant, simple, colour-coded shots like the Edition Two jamun and pomegranate plates, with the
least possible graphics, realistic rather than AI-looking, and creative. Every shot is thought through and made one at a
time.

## Why this style

- **Proof:** the live Edition Two fruit plates, shown with no text, returned 2.75× on Stories, and the ruby anaar plate 2.94×.
  These are the account's best results.
- **What they are:** one honest Indian ingredient whose colour (and, ideally, shape and size) rhymes with the stones, so
  the eye looks twice; a pale natural ground; soft window light; the piece sharp and at true size.
- **Least graphics:** the Story runs with no text at all. The Feed carries only a small tone-on-tone wordmark, a
  three-to-five-word line and the name and price, in a deep shade of the piece's own colour.

## The rules for every shot

1. **One product.** Scene: a ground, one ingredient cluster with a few loose pieces, the jewellery. Nothing else.
2. **The colour rhyme is exact.** The ingredient matches the stones or the metal; where possible it also matches their
   shape and size (arils to baguettes, pods to baguettes, crystals to baguettes).
3. **Elegant grounds only:** ivory lime plaster, sand or sage handwoven linen, kota stone, handmade paper or old brass. Two
   colours at most besides the metal.
4. **Realism:**
   - honest macro optics (the piece needle-sharp, the ingredient behind it softly out of focus);
   - a real contact shadow, real texture and dust, and one small imperfection (a juice stain, a crumb);
   - no CGI gloss and no oversaturation.
5. **True scale.** The ingredient is chosen partly as a size referent, and the prompt states both sizes in mm.
6. **Not weird:** no pair of round things that reads as eyes, no cut fruit with a cavity, nothing clinical, bathroom,
   superstitious, toy-like or footwear.
7. **No repeats.** No pairing that repeats a live Edition Two plate (anaar, elaichi, mishri, mogra, nimbu, churi, haath,
   rain, gath, Ganga-Jamuni, silk, chai, ghadi, jamun, jalebi, dhruv, phuljhadi, monsoon, the Prism diya, saree and kandil).
8. **Products:** the 50 products with 20 or more units in stock (Shopify, 23 Oct). Pearl Circlet Studs stay on hold until
   their listing is confirmed (gold in the copy, silver in every photo).

## Method (per shot)

Look at the listing reference; confirm the construction, stone colour and metal; choose and refine the pairing; write the
prompt in the Edition Two recipe (scene, product lock, scale test, copy space, light, absence list); shoot 9:16 with the
listing packshot in slot 1 and a construction photo in slot 2. Verify at full size and at phone size, reshoot with the
fault named until the score is 8.5 or more, then build the Feed and Story. The log of every shot is in
`build/colour50.json`.
