Rules for every read

SignalRule
Turn offOutbound CTR under 1.2% after 1,000 impressions; or ₹1,200 spent with no add-to-cart; or ₹1,900 spent with no purchase. Never judge before 1,000 impressions or 72 hours.
KeepAnything between the turn-off line and the graduation line.
GraduateROAS 2.1× or more on 2+ purchases, or 1.6× or more on 3+ purchases: duplicate into PERF · Winners with its post.
ScaleWinners budget +20% every 48 hours while its 3-day ROAS stays at 2.0× or more; −20% if it falls under 1.4× over 3 days.
FatigueRetarget frequency over 3.5 in 7 days: swap in the next Quiet ads.

Where the lines come from: last week's average order on Meta was about ₹1,950 (₹7,855 on 4 fruit-plate sales, ₹9,684 on 5 catalogue sales). The Stock Plan puts cash break-even at 1.4× ROAS and profit at 2.1×, so the target cost per purchase is about ₹930 and the ceiling about ₹1,400. ₹1,900 with no sale is twice the target.

Uploading in Ads Manager

  1. In the Ads tab, choose Wave 1 and use Save upload sheet (CSV). It lists every ad with campaign, ad set, wave, primary text, headline, CTA and URL.
  2. Create the four campaigns with the settings above, then their ad sets: Bracelets, Earrings, Necklaces & rings (and the one warm ad set for Quiet).
  3. For each ad: Single image, then "Select media" with placement customisation. Upload the Feed file for Feeds and Explore and the Story file for Stories. Paste the primary text and headline, set Shop now, paste the URL.
  4. Turn off Advantage+ creative enhancements on every ad before publishing.
  5. Publish. Do not edit an ad during its first 72 hours; edits restart learning.

Verification board

Every photograph was checked against the product's reference photo for construction, stone colour and count, metal, true size, face parts and artefacts. Each slot got one reshoot if it failed. Green passed at 8 or more; amber scored 7.5–7.9 and runs only as a reserve. An empty slot shows why it was left empty. Then two independent reviewers checked every finished ad at full size for text over the jewellery, legibility, Story safe zones, line breaks and claims.
