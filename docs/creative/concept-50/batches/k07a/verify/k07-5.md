## Verdict: FAIL, fixable by one regeneration. Choice: none

The hoops themselves are the most faithful Woven render in this run so far. The open chevron plait over a see-through core of finer wires, one round polished disc per hoop on the latch side, the hinge knuckle, the latch wire and its catch, both hoops identical round circles, no stones, no bottom loop, and a clean pale-yellow 18k gold tone. The idea reads, and the gold is the brightest, most saturated thing in a dark stairwell. It fails on two counts:

1. **An invented part on the piece.** Each hoop hangs from a closed, polished **gold loop**, like a jump ring, that rises out of the top of its hinge knuckle and passes up through a hole in the holder's rim (`A_left_top_4x.png`, `A_right_top_4x.png`, circled in `A_loops_marked_1x.png`). The real earring ends at its hinge knuckle (ref 2, `ref03_top_4x.png`). The loop is the same gold tone and polish as the hoop and grows out of the knuckle, so a buyer reads it as part of the earring. That is an added jump ring, a fail under the lock. The brief asked for each latch wire to be hooked into a slot. Instead, the model closed the latch wires into their catches and invented a hanger.
2. **No room for the card in the Story.** The holder and pair fill 36% of the frame height (src y 665-1620) and sit in the middle of it. In the Story the pair lands at y 859-1157, so the planned F2 card (y 1000-1250) would cover its lower half. With only 90 px of wall between the pair and the 1250 text floor, no layout inside the camp's Story zone keeps the card off the piece. The Feed works only at P 68-77, with the headline set over the holders, and the cord tags then have no clean cord to sit on (see section 6).

One regeneration of the same prompt on the same model should fix both: it drew the hoops right. Add the redo clauses at the end of this log.
