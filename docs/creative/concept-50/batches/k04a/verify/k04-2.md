## Verdict: both FAIL. Choice: none

- **Take B** is very close. Its clasp is the best render of this clasp on record: the ring, gate, barrel, nub, ribs and the one-row bar match ref 2 almost exactly. Two things fail it:
  - It invents a **third chain end**. A second strand of links ends in a jump ring against the ring's left side, so three chain ends meet at the clasp. The real piece has two.
  - It sits the clasp **56-63% down** the frame. In the Story that puts it under the card; in the Feed, the ladder column covers the left strand.

  One regeneration of B's prompt, with the clauses at the end, should fix both.
- **Take A** has the wrong ring. It is a generic marine clasp: two ribbed collars, the nub on top, no gate step and no barrel with a jump ring. The bar's jump ring is empty and sits against the ring, with no chain in it. The ring is drawn about 1.4x too big, and the bar is not through the ring, so the planned mid-turn pose did not happen. Do not retry A's pose: leaving the reference-2 pose is what morphs this clasp.
