## Verdict: FAIL. Choice: none (one regeneration should fix it)

The ring itself is well drawn. The single slim S-wave of clear pave cups each green oval on one side and alternates along the front. It never straightens into a row and never closes into a halo, and it matches both references at 2x. The metal is a cool rhodium silver tone and the green is the right hue.

The take fails on three faults that all hit this card's sell, plus a layout problem:

1. **The ring is slipped over her index fingertip.** Her fingertip and nail sit inside the ring's opening, and the bottom of the band passes under the fingertip pad. The prompt says "the ring never on any finger". At Feed size and at 200 px it reads as a ring worn loose on her fingertip. It does not read as a ring standing beside the hand that never needed the thread.
2. **Scale is about 1.9x.** Against her fingernails and fingertips, the ring reads about 37 mm across outside and about 30 mm inside, against 19.5 mm and 16.5 mm. The prompt asked for "about as wide as her fingertip", but it is drawn about 2.5 fingertip widths tall. Together with fault 1, the picture says "too big for her", which is the opposite of an OBJECTION card whose promise is that it fits US 5 to 9.
3. **The open back is not shown.** The gap is there, but it sits at the lower right behind her fingertip and in shadow. Neither looped tip can be read, so at 1x and at Feed size the band reads as a closed full eternity ring. That is the morph risk on the product card, and it hides the exact fact the beat sells: "it opens at the back".
4. **Layout (note).** The hand fills the left 60% of the frame from 29% to 68% down. The prompt asked for a calm left quarter and a dark lower 40%. In the Feed, the ladder, the 2026 break, the gauge caption and the card all land on her hand, the thread or her fingertips.
