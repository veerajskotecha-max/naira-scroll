## Verdict: FAIL. Choice: none (one regeneration should fix it)

The piece itself is the best rendering of this braid so far. The many-wire open plait, the see-through core, the disc, the hinge and the latch all match reference 1 at 4x. The take fails for one reason that layout cannot fix: **scale**. Against her own fingers and thumb, the hoops read about **1.4-1.5x true size, about 35-38 mm instead of 25 mm**. This is the drift the product card warns about, and nothing in the brief intends it. The planned size line, "ABOUT THE TOP JOINT OF HER THUMB", would be visibly false: the hoop is about 1.4x the length of that joint and twice its width. All orders are cash on delivery and there is no return policy, so a hoop that arrives a third smaller than the ad showed is a likely refused parcel.

Three more problems should be fixed in the same regeneration:
- The metal reads amber and oranger than the piece.
- The "faint" haldi became two strongly stained fingertips, and that stain is the largest saturated area in the frame.
- The pair sits too low and the right thumb rises into the type, so the Story card hits the hoops.

## 2. Colour: NOTE (fix in the redo)

- **Metal:** the median hue of the bright, saturated metal is **27 degrees** (interquartile 23-29, saturation 0.73) on both hoops. Refs 01 and 03 measure **34.5 degrees** (interquartile 33-36, saturation 0.60-0.65), so the take is about 7-8 degrees oranger and more saturated. Side by side (`sbs_ref01_1x`, `sbs_ref03_1x`) the take reads as deep amber, a 22k-looking yellow, while the piece is a clean lemon yellow. It still reads as gold tone, never rose, copper or silver tone. This is the same tungsten push that k02-1 found.
- **Disc:** 26 degrees at saturation 0.78, so it reads amber-orange. The ref 03 disc is a pale butter yellow (38 degrees, saturation 0.33, value 0.91).
- There are no stones, so there is no clear-stone check.
- **Haldi competes with the gold.** Two fingertips at the left are stained strong orange-yellow, with a light stain on a third. Their colour (hue 25-28 degrees, saturation 0.73) is the same as the metal's, and their saturated area is larger: about 40,000 px against about 28,000 px for both hoops together. That breaks the camp rule "the piece is always the only saturated thing". At 200 px the eye lands on the fingertips first.

## 3. Scale: FAULT (unintended, about 1.4-1.5x)

- **Bounding boxes (src px):**
  - Lying hoop: x 505-783 (278 px) by y 1291-1515, with the latch top at about y 1265.
  - Standing hoop: x 845-948 by y 1203 (hinge top) to 1520. The braid runs y 1235-1520, which is 285 px.

  - Diameter used: **282 px**.
- **Feed px per mm:** 282 x 0.703125 / 25 = **7.9**. That is under 19, so no "Shown magnified" line is due. The plan assumed about 12.
- **Against her hands (the brief's referent, which touches the piece):**
