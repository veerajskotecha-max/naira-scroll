## Verdict: FAIL. Choice: none (one regeneration should fix it)

The construction is mostly right:
- There are 2 full turns, with 2 wires above the caps and 2 below.
- At the caps there are 3 wires: one cap starts a wire, the middle wire passes between the caps, and the other cap ends a wire.
- The caps sit on different rows and are offset along the wire. This is not the "third coil" read, and it is not a stack of separate bangles.
- The metal tone is very close to both references.

The take fails on two lock items. Both are morphs the product card names:

1. **The pavé is redesigned.** Each cap carries a micro-pavé field of about 35-45 tiny stones (about 8-9 across and 5 down) inside a thin raised frame. The lock and both references have **8 large round stones in 2 columns of 4**, each in its own setting (`sbs_caps_take8x_ref01_4x`, `sbs_caps_take8x_ref03_8x`). The product card's morph note warns of exactly this ("a solid pavé band").
2. **The coil is about 1.5x too chunky.** The wire is 26.5 src px thick on a bangle about 303 px across, so the diameter is **11.4 wire widths**. Reference 1 measures 17.4 (868 / 50 px), reference 2 about 18 (300 / 16.5 px), and the listing gives 68 / 4 = 17. Against her index finger (about 68 px, about 15 mm), the bangle's diameter is right (about 68 mm). The wire, though, reads **about 6 mm instead of 4**, the two turns read **about 12 mm instead of 8**, and the caps read about 10 mm instead of 8. On a cash-on-delivery order with no return policy, a slimmer wire than the ad showed is a likely refused parcel. The size line "8 MM ACROSS THE TWO TURNS · ABOUT A PENCIL'S THICKNESS" would also be visibly false beside this render.

The take also misses the brief in four ways. These are not hard-rule faults, but they cost the idea:
- **There are four steel vessels, not three tiers.** A taller fourth container stands behind the tap, in addition to the held one and the two on the board. At 200 px you count four, which muddles "three tiers, three people" and the BUY 3 device.
- **No droplets on the bangle.** It is dry, so the waterproof story is told only by the room.
- **The bangle is small in the frame:** 13% of the frame width, against the 40% asked, and the field of view is about 33 cm. The prompt itself conflicts here: three 11 cm containers cannot fit in a 15 cm field, and the model kept the containers.
- **The mustard sleeve is not muted.** It measures hue 38, saturation 0.53, value 0.61, against the metal's 33.6 / 0.52 / 0.67. So the gold is not "the only warm, saturated, bright thing", and at 200 px the sleeve is the biggest warm mass.
