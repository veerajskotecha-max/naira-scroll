**Not faithful.** The pave link (the hero) is right. The chain around it is a different chain: separate connector rings, half the links and slimmer tube links. A buyer would receive a heavier and denser bracelet than this one, which is the opposite of the "bold" this ad sells. The construction rule ("anything invented, missing or redesigned is a fail") decides it.

Cause: the prompt asked for "every link flat and touching the next", and for a pave link plus 3 links each side on a 9 cm card inside a 10 cm frame. At the real interlocked pitch (about 14-15 mm a link) those numbers cannot all hold. The model kept the count and the straight line, and filled the joints with connectors.
