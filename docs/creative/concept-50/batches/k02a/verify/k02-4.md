## Verdict: FAIL (fixable with one regeneration). Choice: none

The piece itself is right. The left earring is the barrel face from ref 01. The right one is the C with its smooth inner face, as in the listing macro. Both are brushed satin 18k gold tone with no stones, hinge, post or ridge. There is no face, no legible text and no extra jewellery. The take fails on **scale against the brief's own referent**. Each earring stands **taller than the cassette is thick** (343 px against a 25 mm front edge of about 280 px). That puts the pair at about **1.9x** its real size next to the tape, as if each earring were about 26 x 19 mm. The brief asked for "about life size, 14 mm against the cassette's 25 mm thickness" and calls the cassette "an honest scale referent". The product card names blown-up scale as this piece's second risk. With no return policy behind it, a picture that sells a 14 mm earring as an inch-tall one is a fault, and type cannot own it as a joke. Secondary faults: the **Story lower third covers the bottom 15-22 px of both earrings**, there is no crisp 1996 hard shadow, and a rose band from the silk is reflected on the left earring. One regeneration with the clauses below should fix all of these.

## 3. Scale and size line: FAULT (unintended, about 1.9x against the cassette)

- **Bounding box (src px):** pair x 485-1170, y 1127-1478 (left piece x 485-750, y 1127-1468; right piece x 815-1170, y 1130-1478). The pair spans **44.6%** of the width (the brief asked for about 45%) and sits **41-54%** down the frame (the brief asked for 40-50%).
- **Feed px per mm:** left barrel 343 px for 14 mm, so 343 x 0.703125 / 14 = **17.2**. That is under 19, so no "Shown magnified" line. On a phone (1080 Feed px across about 65-71 mm of glass, 15-16.6 px per mm) the earrings display at about **1.0-1.1x life size**, so the card's `14 × 9.8 MM · SHOWN ABOUT LIFE SIZE` is true on screen.
- **Against the referent (the cassette):** the brief's scale is "a little over half the 25 mm thickness of the cassette". Two measurements on the cassette agree:
  - **Thickness.** The front edge under the pair (knurled rim to the bottom edge, carrying the cream label) is about **270-290 px**, and the left earring is **343 px** (`A_scale_ruler_1x.png`). Both are vertical surfaces seen from the same camera, so the earring should look about 0.56x the edge, or about 0.7x after allowing for the barrel top seen from above. It looks **1.2x**.
  - **Depth.** The top face is about 905 px from front to back edge for 103 mm, against 280 px for 25 mm thick. That puts the camera about 38 degrees up and the front plane at about **14.2 px/mm**. The earrings stand behind that plane, so they should draw at about 14 px/mm or a little less. They draw at **24.5 px/mm** vertically (26 mm implied after the top-reveal correction) and **27 px/mm** across (265 px for 9.8 mm, so 18.7 mm implied).
  - **Result:** each earring reads about **1.9x** its real size next to the tape, roughly 26 x 19 mm. That is far past the 30% line, and nothing in the brief intends it. At 200 px the earrings look as tall as the cassette is thick (`A_200.png`). A buyer who has handled VHS tapes would read them as about an inch tall. The product card's own warning has come true ("the wide band gets ... blown up into a large hoop").
- The model also shot the cassette from about 38 degrees, not "low at about 15 degrees", and squeezed it: its left end and most of its depth fit in the frame. That is how the pair got big against it.
- **Size line if it shipped:** `14 × 9.8 MM · SHOWN ABOUT LIFE SIZE` (no magnified line at 17.2). The words are true on screen, but the picture contradicts them against the tape. A layout fallback cannot make this honest, so I do not recommend shipping this take with a stronger size line.
