### V1 expert worst issues

- **perf1:** Regenerate the worn frame at true size, a 29 mm drop at about half the ear's height, and crop closer so the earring is the brightest, largest thing inside the heart spotlight. The heart frame should spotlight the piece, not the ear.
- **perf2:** Rebuild the photo at true size inside the same heart vignette. The idea is the best of the three, but the frame oversells the piece by about 40-50% while printing '29 mm'. Next, swap the concept-noun headline for one that names the outcome, for example 'Your own heart frame.'
- **craft1:** Too many words fight the frame. Cut the two arc labels and push the vignette much harder, with a deeper and cleaner surround and star-filter glints on the rim, so the heart frame reads at 200 px and the earring is the brightest thing in it. As it is, the maroon wash muddies the skin and the heart barely separates from the ground.
- **craft2:** Give the Feed the Story's composition. Draw a whole heart fully inside the frame (the Feed cuts off the left lobe), drop the two arched captions, and limit the maroon wash to a soft mask outside the heart so the skin stays clean. The heart shape is the whole idea and has to read at a glance.
- **truth1:** Same oversized worn picture, about 1.45x against the ear, so a true-size regeneration is needed. Second, with more than four text elements (two arc labels, the REC label, the size line, the headline and the sub-line), the one-message cap applies. Cut the arc labels to one, add 'cubic zirconia', and remove free shipping.
- **truth2:** It is the same 1.3-1.5x oversized worn picture under a '29 MM DROP · top joint of a thumb' size line. Regenerate the ear shot at true size. In the Story, also move the heart vignette so it doesn't cut the ear in half and darken its top.

Note on these: "remove free shipping" (truth1, truth2) is overruled by the owner ruling of 20 Nov 2026, so the line stays. The scale point stands for the jury: all six runs read the drop at about 1.35-1.5x against the ear. The verifier measured the same drop/ear ratio (0.69) but read it against the listing's own worn photos (0.57 on true-scale photo 02, 0.78 on photo 05), about 1.2x and inside the 30% line, which is why the plate reached design with the 29 mm line printed on both formats.
