## 5. Idea and stop power

**The brief:** "The winning hand rises out of the paraat and holds the pair up like a cup ... under a phoolon ki baarish ... the earring itself is the scoreboard: three clear rounds, then the prize."

**Take A.**
- What reads: the bride's mehendi fingertips, just out of the milk, present the pair like a trophy on a plate, with milk threads falling back into the brass paraat.
- At 200 px the "three small lights, then one of mint" pattern reads on both earrings, so the scoreboard idea and ROUNDS TO PRIZE are drawn on a truthful piece.
- What is thin: the petal shower is only 6 petals. The baarish reads as "a few petals", not ticker-tape, and there is no milk crown. The Kling motion (a drifting shower, the milk threads falling) has to carry the podium.
- Scores: crazy **6**, stop200 **8**. Big, bright mint on oxblood, the mehendi and the milk threads stop the thumb.

- Scores: crazy **6**, stop200 **6**.
