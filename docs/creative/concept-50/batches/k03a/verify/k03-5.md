## 3. Scale

  - The index fingertip is about 95-100 src px wide, against about 15 mm on an adult woman's index finger. The thumb nail is about 100 px long, against about 13-15 mm. Both give about 6.5-7 src px per mm.

- **Result:** an unintended mis-scale of more than 30% (about 2x). The brief does not play with scale (field of view about 9 cm, true size against the thumb). A buyer would see a 4 cm earring in her fingers.
