### V3 expert worst issues

- **perf1:** Re-render the ring with the real fine ridge pitch (about 1.3 mm, about 60 ridges round). The x1.5 inset magnifies coarse, spring-like ribs at about twice the real pitch, so the hero close-up is the least truthful part of the ad. That keeps truth under the ship rule's 8.
- **perf2:** Make the image name her pain, such as a stone lost mid-function or the ring taken off and lost. Right now 'nothing to lose' plus a list of what the ring lacks only implies PAIN, and 'only the milk falls off' depends on a paraat the Feed crop hides.
- **craft1:** The proof is off-frame in the feed. 'only the milk falls off' points at a faint streak on the palm, while the paraat splash (the image that would get the ad talked about, visible in the Story) is buried under the card. Compact or lower the card, or move the window, so the splash shows. On a reshoot, also seat the ring at the base of the finger, not in the midi position.
- **craft2:** Put her pain on the canvas. 'no stones, no clasp, no gap.' reads as a reassurance (OBJECTION), not a PAIN. Swap it for the Meta line 'Lost a stone mid-sangeet?' so 'NOTHING TO LOSE.' answers a problem she recognises. Also fix the Story line break 'no gap. only / the milk falls off.'
- **truth1:** Remove 'FREE INSURED SHIPPING' (blocker). Creatively, the Feed crop buries the milk paraat under the card, so 'only the milk falls off' has no visible proof in Feed. Frame the Feed lower or move the card so the splash shows.
- **truth2:** Delete 'FREE INSURED SHIPPING' from the card (RULINGS §2), since it is the only compliance blocker. After that, the truth fix is the ridges: the inset magnifies a ring with about 40 chunky ridges where the real piece has about 60 fine ones at 1.3 mm, so the 2x view looks coarser than the piece.

Note on the truth and perf runs: "Remove FREE INSURED SHIPPING (blocker)" is overruled by the owner ruling of 20 Nov 2026, so the line stays. The ridge point is a perceptual one about the ×1.5 loupe; the verifier counted 17-19 ridges on the visible face, matching worn ref 2 (about 18), and measured the band-to-finger ratio inside true size.

### Why V3 won

- It is the only variant where the piece reads at 200 px: the ring inside the O of NOTHING is the hero at headline size, while V1 and V2 show an 8%-wide gold glint on a fist (200 px mean 6.83 against 5.58 and 4.67).
- Every customer, in both runs, named T9 their favourite and five of six would buy it (buy yes 0.83, against 0.33 and 0); they cite the visible ridges, ₹999 and cash on delivery.
- It leads on expert mean (6.91 against 6.54 and 5.83) and has no dim under 6; its one loss is message (6.17): the canvas reassures ("no stones, no clasp, no gap") rather than naming her pain.
- It beats the winner anchor apricot on run 1 but not the loser anchors, and truth stays at 7.17, so it is held, not shipped.

### Fixes for a polish pass (no new picture needed for the first three)

1. Name the pain on the canvas: customers and critics both asked for V3's loupe with V2's line. Swap the beat for 'the ring you take off to wash is the one that goes missing.' or 'lost a stone mid-sangeet?', keeping NOTHING TO LOSE. as the answer (Meera 1 and 2 say this exact combination is the one they would buy tonight).
2. Show the proof in the Feed: compact or lower the F2 card so the milk splash and paraat read under it (critics say the Feed hides it).
3. Story line break: set the beat as 'no stones, / no clasp, / no gap. / only the milk falls off.' as in the Feed, not 'no gap. only / the milk falls off.'
4. Truth to 8: put a plain 1:1 detail crop (×1.0 to ×1.2) in the O instead of ×1.5 so the ridge pitch reads as fine as the piece, or reshoot with the verifier's optional polish (back of the hand to camera, ring on the ring finger, field of view about 12 cm).
