## Faults and notes (take A)

1. **Composition vs brief.** The barrel's centre is at 70% of the height (not centred) and its width is 12.4% of the frame (not 18%). The plan's layout coordinates collide with the chain; use layout (a) or (b) above. This is a layout note, not a fail.
2. **Size line.** At 12.1 Feed px per mm, drop "SHOWN MAGNIFIED". Print `BARREL ABOUT 10 MM · BOX CHAIN 2.5 MM · 50 CM`.
3. **Pavé.** It is slightly coarser than ref 1: the outer bands read as one row of larger stones with melee, not two even rows of tiny stones. This is invisible at ad scale (the barrel is about 134 Feed px). The structure (3 bands, 2 ribs, rims, bore) is exact.
4. **Flash signature.** It is soft, with medium-soft shadows. The metal is a touch deeper than ref 1 but has the same hue (30.6° vs 32.0°). This does not misstate the piece.
5. **Background.** There is an out-of-focus cloth fold behind the potli at src (100-200, 250-330). It reads as fabric and is outside Feed (P 40 or more) and under Story's top UI band.

All five are fixable by one regeneration if ever wanted. None needs one.
