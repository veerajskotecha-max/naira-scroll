- **Against the plan:** the plan asked for a 6.5 cm field with each hoop at 30% of the frame width (16.6 Feed px per mm). The take shows each hoop at 36.6% (about a 5.5 cm field). That is +19-22%, inside the 30% tolerance, and the plan already intends a magnified hoops-on-floor view.
