There is one take, and it fails on two counts, both fixable by one regeneration.

1. **The bar has grown.** It measures **0.87x the ring**. The lock allows 0.6-0.7x, and the real photos show 0.56-0.60x. On this plate's own true-scale jadval ruler, the bar would measure about 19 mm, so the planned roundel "STONE-SET BAR · ABOUT 15 MM" would contradict the frame.
2. **The clasp is too low and too far right.**
   - The ring's centre is 51% down and 84% across, and its bottom is at 56%. The brief's band is 26-53%.
   - In the Story, the F2 cartouche (y 1000-1250) covers the bottom of the ring and the chain's dip, and there is no room under the piece for the caption.
   - In both crops, the push-pin nub runs to within 2-4 px of the album frame's inner rule and its ruler ticks.

Everything else is strong:
- the ring is a real sailor ring (gate seams, ribbed collar, nub), not a donut or a lobster;
- there are exactly two chain ends;
- the metal is one 18k gold tone and the stones are colourless;
- there are no people, no text and no water;
- the riderless procession with drums and no drummers is exactly as briefed.

The same model held this clasp in the bake-off, and the faults are proportion and placement, not a misunderstanding. So one regeneration with the clauses at the end should fix it.
