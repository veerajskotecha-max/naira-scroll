## Verdict: FAIL. Choice: none

There is one take. **The piece itself is the best construction in the batch**: the pair is close to a re-staging of ref 02, with the taper, the hinge wire, the knob, the seam and the inner channel all correct. It fails on the world and the colour around the piece, and both are fixable by one regeneration.

1. **The painting has become a 3D toy city: the brief's named risk 2.**
   - The prompt asks for "perfectly flat" paper where "the painted domes are brushstrokes, not models".
   - What came back is a painterly 3D diorama. The domes and pavilions are modelled volumes with cast shadows. The foreground has tilt-shift blur, and the scale changes with distance: a foreground dome is at least 12 mm wide at the hoop's scale, against 3-5 mm domes on the skyline.
   - A painted dome finial stands **in front of** the right hoop's foot. Paint on a flat page cannot do that.
   - As a result the picture reads as "giant earrings in a miniature city", the familiar CGI trope, not "a real piece lying on a folio".
   - **It also breaks the album.** Cards 1-4 are flat, sparse, flat-colour pages (`series_compare.png`), and this card's own Feed is a spread. Window A (this plate) sits beside windows B and C, which are crops of the flat pages of cards 2 and 4. A diorama next to two flat folios is visibly a different campaign. The camp's honesty line ("the piece is never enlarged; the world is painted small") stops being visible in the picture.
2. **The saffron world has dyed the metal.**
   - On the ring mask, the take's metal is hue **29.6°**, saturation **0.89**, value 0.78 (median RGB 200/111/23). That hue is exactly the painted sky's (29.6°).
   - Ref 02 is hue **33.9°**, saturation **0.55**, value 0.84 (median RGB 213/162/92).
   - So the listing's light honey-yellow 18k gold tone shows as **deep orange-amber**. A quarter of the take's metal pixels sit below hue 28°, against 11% in ref 02.
   - The metal is also **darker than the paint** around it (sky value 0.95, ground 0.91). That breaks the camp's palette rule: "the piece is always the brightest, most saturated thing… gold tone on saffron only where the kicker makes the metal brighter than the paint".
   - On a cash-on-delivery store with no return policy, an ad that shows the piece more orange than it arrives is a refusal risk. Grading the piece afterwards is not allowed, so this needs a regeneration.
3. **The foreground below 54% is not calm.**
   - It is dense blurred architecture with two big domes, not the calm painted ground the composition asks for.
   - Cards set on solid cartouches still work, but the picture is the busiest in the series, and gold on gold leaves the stop power at 6.

Notes, not faults on their own:
- the hoops are 33% of the frame width each, against the 24% asked (1.39x), which pushes the full-width Feed to 22.3 px per mm;
- the three cranes fly level to the right, not toward the hoops, and are drawn birds with dot eyes rather than silhouettes;
- the dome finial overlaps about 18 x 28 src px of the front hoop's lowest edge.

No hard rule is broken: no faces, no text, no water, no flags, no second piece, construction exact. If no redo credit is left, A can ship as a fallback, with the layout in section 6. It is not the 10/10 the camp is built for.
