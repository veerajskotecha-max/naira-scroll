## 1. Construction and counts (against 01 and 03)

There is no fault. The take copies the reference hoop almost exactly (`side_pair_1x.png`).
- **Braid:** a many-wire open plait. Polished round wires run in pairs and cross in chevrons over a core of finer polished wires with dark gaps, and the coral wall shows through in places. It is not a smooth tube, a 2-strand twist, a mesh or glitter fill. The braid is about 140 src px thick against a true diameter of about 525 px, a ratio of 0.27. Ref 03 gives 0.26.
- **Discs:** 2, one per hoop, flat, round and mirror-polished, on the front of the braid at the top on the latch side. The front disc is 150 x 165 src px, about 7-8 mm, so about a third of the hoop's width, as in both refs. There is no second disc on either hoop.
- **Hinge knuckle:** the front hoop shows its knuckle at the top left, with the pin dot, as in refs 01 and 03. The back hoop's knuckle is hidden behind the front hoop's catch, which is consistent with the 125 px offset.
- **Latch wires:** 1 per hoop. The front wire runs from the knuckle to its catch. The back wire passes behind the nail head (resting on the shank), through its catch, and sticks out a little beyond it with a rounded end, as in ref 01.
- **Stones:** 0. **Bottom loop:** none (`A_bottom_2x.png`). **Extra elements:** none.
- **Pose:** one hoop just behind the other, offset about 6 mm right and slightly up, as the prompt asks.

Notes (not faults):
- **Shape.** Each hoop measures about 455 x 525 src px (front) and 455 x 515 (back), so it is about 1.15 times taller than wide. This is the same three-quarter turn both listing photos show (ref 01 is about 1.2 and ref 03 about 1.2 by the same measure), and the left side of the braid reads wider than the right, as a turned hoop does. It reads as a round hoop turned a little on the nail, not as an oval redesign. The prompt asked for perfect circles, so this is a note. The truer 25 mm reading is the vertical diameter (525 px).
- **Catch.** Each catch is a closed oval eye on top of its disc. In both refs the eye carries a small forked notch on top that the latch wire clicks into (`side_catch_3x.png`). The notch is about 2 mm, roughly 25 Feed px, and no shopper reads it at feed size, so this is a simplification, not a redesign.
- **Glints:** two small four-point star glints sit on the left braid. They are on metal, not stones, but they look slightly CG. They are harmless.
- **Hanging:** the front hoop's latch meets the nail head at its lower left, so how it is supported is a little ambiguous at 2x. At Feed size the pair reads as hung on one brass nail. The nail head is matte, brushed brass, darker than the polish, so it does not read as part of the earring.

## 5. The idea at 200 px

- **Crazy 7, stop at 200 px 8** (`A_200.png`, `feed_P50_200.png`). At thumbnail size you see a bright gold-tone pair, then a ring of small dancing women hand in hand in its shadow, with skirts and raised linked arms. It reads as people, not a flower or a cog. The pair is the brightest, most saturated thing in the frame, as the camp's commercial insight wants.
- **Notes against the brief** (no regeneration needed for any of them):
  - **Count:** there are 7 dancers, not the 8 asked. No copy states a number, so nothing is untrue.
  - **Orientation:** all the dancers are drawn upright (heads up), not radially with feet to the centre and heads outward. The ring still reads as people.
  - **Costume:** the skirts are knee to calf-length flared frocks with legs showing. They read as a folk or children's ring dance more than lehenga-clad sangeet dancers. The type-shadow "and the whole sangeet, for the cousin." carries the sangeet.
  - **The braid's wires as linked arms:** this reads only loosely. The arms are linked overhead all round the ring.
- **Physics:** sun from the left, low. The specular edges sit on the left of the wires, the shadows fall to the right and slightly down, and the hoops keep a true contact shadow before it turns into the ring. The nail throws its own short wedge. Consistent.

## Flags for the finisher and motion (not picture faults)

1. **Use ink, not bone** for all type on this plate: the wall is a light salmon, where bone is 2.0:1 and ink 7.7:1. Use a coral-deep offer lozenge.
2. **Type-shadow multiply at about 40-45%**, not 72%, to match the plate's shadow.
3. **Replace the card-5 layout coordinates** with section 6. The pair is about 85 px lower and about 1.5x larger than the plan assumed.
4. **Motion:** the dancers are drawn upright, not radially. Rotating a masked copy of the ring by a quarter turn per bar (the plan's edit fallback) would tip them onto their sides and upside down. Turn the ring by advancing each figure to the next slot while it stays upright, or let Kling animate the dance with the hoops locked. Never move the hoops or the nail.
