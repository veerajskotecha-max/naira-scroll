## Verdict: FAIL. Choice: none (one regeneration should fix it)

The hard part is right: the take has 2 full turns, and the 3-wire stack sits only between the 2 offset caps. The upper cap ends the top turn at the front right, and the lower cap ends the bottom turn at the front left, the same arrangement as refs 01, 02 and 03. It is not 3 coils and not a stack of separate bangles. The idea also lands: one coil on the arm, a stack of bangles on the shadow arm.

It fails on two lock items. Both are the morphs the product card warns about, and both would be contradicted by this ad's own copy:

1. **Pavé count: 6 stones per cap, not 8.** Both caps carry 2 columns of 3. The lock and refs 01, 02 and 03 all show 8 stones in 2 columns of 4 (`sbs_caps_take4x_ref01_3x`). The Feed callout planned for this card reads "clear cubic zirconia, 8 each", so the picture would contradict its own proof line. The caps are also redesigned. Each is a flat, near-square plate with a raised bevel frame, about 1.6 wire widths tall. The reference caps are solid mirror-polished blocks, about 2 wire widths tall, taller than they are long, with a rounded tip.
2. **The wire is about 1.6 to 1.8 times too thick.** The two turns read about 13 to 15 mm, not 8 mm (details in section 3). This is the same chunky-coil drift that failed k04-5A. On a cash-on-delivery order with no published return policy, a parcel that arrives slimmer than the ad is a refusal risk. The brief's own scale line ("the two turns are 8 mm tall, as tall as a pencil is thick") is also visibly false here.
