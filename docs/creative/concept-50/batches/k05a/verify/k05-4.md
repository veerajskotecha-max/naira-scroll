## 5. Idea and stop power

The brief's idea is delivered: a gold-tone snake chain whose line becomes a cobra on the wall, keeping one point of white light in its hood (`A_piece_and_cobra_feedscale.png`). It reads in one look because:
- the chain and the snake are one continuous S;
- the jewel is the brightest point in the shadow;
- the real stone below is the brightest thing on the face.

Two departures from the plan's picture text, neither a fault:
- **The cobra rises from the chain, not from the pendant.** This follows the take's own prompt ("the line of chain shadow thickens into a cobra"). The pendant keeps its own honest long shadow on the face, which is the camp's "true contact shadow first" rule.
- **The cobra is about 1.5 to 2x the pendant, not giant** (N8). The hood reads as a cobra (a rounded spread hood above a thin rising neck) more than as a generic snake. But a bigger, higher hood would hit harder.

Scores:
- **Stop at 200 px: 7.** At 200 px wide (full plate, Feed at P 36 and Story) the blaze of the stone, the gold S of the chain and the hooded shadow with its star all read. The pale apricot wall makes the frame gentler than the planned terracotta, and the lower half is empty plaster (by design, for the card).
- **Crazy: 7.** A clean, mythic shadow-art idea with a physical wit (the snake chain literally turns into the snake). It is executed faithfully but a size short of "giant".

## Faults

None that need a regeneration.

Notes, in order of weight:
1. **N1, the chain runs out in frame.** The left run fades into defocus at the rear of the parapet top. The right run's last few millimetres turn into the root of the cobra shadow. This is the brief's intended device and adds no element, but the owner should see it, given the camp's "never replaces the piece" line.

3. **N4, stone against chain.** The stone renders 6.4 chain widths against the listing's 5 to 5.5 (16 to 28% large). That is inside tolerance, and the size line states 10 mm.
4. **N3 and N5, prongs and facets.** The prong balls are about 1.4x heavier than the listing's. The facets are a regular radiating cushion pattern, not crushed ice. Both are still a 4-prong cushion.
5. **N6, stone mid-tones.** The stone's mid-tones carry a warm reflection of the plaster (sat 0.19 against 0.10). The highlights are white and it reads clear.

7. **N8, cobra size.** The cobra is about 1.5 to 2x the pendant, not "many times" bigger. Its idea still reads at 200 px.
