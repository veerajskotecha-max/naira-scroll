## Verdict: choice none. Take A fails, and one regeneration can fix it.

The ring itself is the most faithful this piece has been rendered in any batch:
- both layers are there;
- the flag and point outline is exact;
- all 4 windows show skin;
- the pavé is at true stone size;
- the lilac matches ref 01 within 4° of hue.

Two faults remain:
1. **The butterfly is turned 90° on its shank.** The body bar runs across the finger, and the shank runs straight on from the end of the body bar. So the 24 mm wingspan lies along the finger instead of across it. On the real ring the shank leaves at right angles to the body bar: in ref 01 it runs left-right behind the lower wings, ref 02 shows it going off sideways, and in ref 05 the body bar lies along the finger. The product card says the same: "the wingspan runs across the finger with the body bar along it". This changes the construction where the head joins the shank, and it misstates how the ring sits worn. Across the finger it reads 1.1 finger widths, against a true 1.4.
2. **The frame is about 1.55x tighter than asked, so there is no clean room for type in the Feed.** The ring is 17% of the frame width against "about a tenth". The hand runs from 29% to 63% down the frame, and its finger shadows reach 70%. The prompt asked for both to end above half-way. No Feed P holds the headline column and the F2 card with the trust line without type crossing skin or shadow.
