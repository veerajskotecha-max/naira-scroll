Take A is faithful to the real ring. The idea reads at once: a gold tone two-stone ring on a ledge, and on the wall two shadow people holding hands, with a point of light where their hands meet. No regeneration is needed. Every note below is for the layout.

## 1. Construction and counts (against 02 and 01)

The construction is the same as in ref 02, almost pose for pose. No fault.
- **Band:** 1 smooth, round, high-polish wire, open bypass. The loop reaches left from the bottom of the tiger eye's bezel, comes round the front and rises back to that bezel. The band's other end runs behind the tiger eye and comes out at its right as a thin wire that drops into the top of the crystal's bezel. This is the same path as in ref 02. The band is not closed, flattened or thickened: the wire is about 30 src px against a tiger eye of about 250 px, a ratio of about 0.12, and ref 02 gives about 0.10 to 0.13.
- **Stones:** 2 cushion stones, set diagonally, corners nearly touching. The tiger eye is upper left and the crystal lower right, as in ref 02.
  - **Tiger eye:** opaque banded golden-brown with chatoyant bands. Its top has a few soft, irregular facet lines, with no checkerboard. It is not glassy and is not a twin of the crystal.
  - **Crystal:** transparent, faceted, pale champagne.
- **Bezels:** 2 slim, plain bezels in the same gold tone. The visible wall on the left of each bezel is perspective, as in ref 02.
- **Nothing added:** 0 pavé (no false "halo"), 0 prongs, no jump ring, no extra element.
- **Pose:** the ring lies on its band, stones up, in the wide cube pose of ref 02, not "upright" as the card's picture text asks. The product card prefers this pose because it holds the extent. Accepted.

## 5. Idea and stop power

The brief's idea is delivered:
- The ring casts two people leaning in, holding hands.
- The physics joke works. The right head is solid. The left head is visibly lit from within, because the crystal lets light through. One small champagne caustic glows exactly where the hands meet, at src about (1070, 742), Feed about (760, 426) at P 18, and Story about (762, 530). The type's lit "aur" will rhyme with it.
- The light direction is consistent: the sun is from the left, the ring's own ledge shadow (the loop ellipse plus the crystal's caustic spray) falls to the right, and the figures rise on the wall to the right.

One weakness, which is not a fault:
- The figures rise from the far end of the ring's true ledge shadow. They do not grow continuously out of the ring's outline, and the heads cannot be traced to the stones.
- The still reads as "the ring's shadow" because of adjacency and the shared light. The motion edit (the shadow stretching from its true outline) supplies the growth.

Scores:
- **Stop at 200 px: 8.** At 200 px wide, both the full plate and the Feed crop read in a glance: a blaze of gold tone, two stones, and a couple in shadow with a light at the hands.
- **Crazy: 7.** It is a clean, poetic shadow-art idea, executed without compromise. It is not absurd, but it is fresh for the category.

## Faults

Notes for the layout and the motion edit:
1. **Scale and pose:** the ring is about 1.3 to 1.6 x the planned share and lies on its band (the ref 02 pose). Neither is a fault, and it is still under 19 Feed px/mm.
2. **Feed placement:** the Feed needs P = 18%, which puts the ring's bottom 26 px past the 930 gate. The strict alternative, P = 22.5%, crops the right head's crown at the top edge.
3. **Moved type and card:** the plan's Feed size line (y 790), Story size line (y 968) and Story card (y 1010 to 1250) all land on the ring. They move to the ledge lip and ledge face as above.
4. **Headline width:** the 132 px headline must break into two lines, and the type-shadow must stay about 80 to 84 px, to keep clear of the figures.

6. **Colours:** the wall is deeper than the #9EC3D6 token and the ledge is warm sand rather than bone. Keep ink type on both.
