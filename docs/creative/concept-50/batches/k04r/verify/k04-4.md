This re-shoot is a big step forward. In k04a the earrings came out 3x true size; here A is about 1.4x and B about 1.8x.

It still fails, narrowly, on the fault that held this card twice before: **scale**.
- **How big:** against her fingernails and fingertips, which are the referents at the earrings' own depth, each earring measures **about 1.35-1.5x true size**. The barrel face is wider than her fingernail, where a real 9.8 mm piece is a little narrower than one.

- **Why it matters:** the size line "14 MM TALL" sits next to hands that say about 20 mm. Every order is cash on delivery with no return policy, so a piece that arrives smaller than the ad showed is a refused-parcel risk.

**Take B** fails scale clearly, at **about 1.8x**: the barrel face is wider than her fingertip. The prompt asked for each fingertip to be wider than either earring. B is also off-brief in two ways:
