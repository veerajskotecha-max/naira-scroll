## 3. Scale

- **Referent (ear), `A_ear_grid.png`:** the helix top is at y 568 and the lobe tip at about y 1135, so the ear is **567 px** tall.
  - The hoop is **254 / 567 = 0.45** of the ear's height (ring height 280 gives 0.49).
  - A true 25 mm hoop on a 55-62 mm adult ear would be 0.40-0.45 of it.
  - So the hoop is about **1.0-1.1x** true. That is well inside the 30% line and fixes the k01a drift (0.59, 0.62). The prompt's "a little less than half the ear's height" landed.
- **Source px per mm:** about 10.8. The disc's long axis gives 85 / 8 = 10.6, the ring's height 280 / 25 = 11.2, and the foreshortened width 254 / 25 = 10.2.
- **Field:** 1520 / 10.8 = about **14 cm** wide, against the 11 cm asked. The hoop is 16.7% of the frame's width, against the plan's 21-25%. It is smaller in frame but true against the body, which is the camp's promise ("true size outside").
- **Feed px per mm** (k = 1080 / 1520 = 0.7105): 10.8 x 0.7105 = **7.7** outside the lens (7.2-8.0 across the three measures). Inside the HTML 2x lens it is about **15.4**. The Story (scale 0.7143) is about the same.
- **Size line:** keep the planned scale tag, **"IN HER LENS ABOUT 2× · ACTUAL 25 MM ACROSS"**.
  - Outside the lens the hoop is under 19 Feed px per mm and is not shown giant, so no "Shown magnified" line is needed. The lens magnification is owned by the tag.
  - The plan's "about 19.6 px per mm" inside the lens does not hold on this plate (2k, 14 cm field). Do not print a px figure. If the designer wants 19 or more inside the lens, a 2.5x lens gives about 19.3, and the tag must then read "ABOUT 2.5×". At 2.5x the crop upsamples about 1.78 Feed px per source px and goes softer, so I recommend keeping 2x.
