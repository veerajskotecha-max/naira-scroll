## Verdict: PASS WITH NOTES. Choice: **A**.

This is the most faithful Halo Curve picture the camp has had. The construction matches ref 01 almost line for line, and the colour and the scale against the finger are true. The staging faults of k01a are all gone:
- The glasses are flat-topped D half-moons, held folded beside the ring.

- The ring sits at 45% of the frame's height, not in the card zone.

The chai rhyme lands in the photograph itself: the clear crystal sits right over milky brown tea in a clear ribbed glass, with the brown tiger eye beside it.

The notes are minor and layout-solvable:
1. The crystal is about 17% narrower than the tiger eye.
2. The kettle's lid reaches into the bottom of the headline band on the left.
3. The aunty's hand and glasses occupy the right callout gutter.
4. The lower fingers are lit, so the band text needs the scrim to start higher.
5. The plate is 2k, so the HTML 2x lens upsamples about 1.42x.

The picture would be fixable by one regeneration if wanted, but no redo is needed.
