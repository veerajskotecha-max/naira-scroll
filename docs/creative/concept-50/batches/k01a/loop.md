## 3. Fix target

**Lowest dimension across the batch: message, 5.56.** That is the mean of k01-4's three variations (5.67, 5.67 and
5.33), and it is the only dimension under 6 on all three. On the chosen V2:

- fit (5.33) is lower still, and message (5.67) comes second;
- the critics' message scores were perf 5.5, craft 6 and truth 5.5;
- fit and message have one cause.

**Why it is low.** Each variation broke "one message" a different way:

- **V2:** the headline sells weight ("BHAARI HAI. ACHHA HAI.", 27.6 g), but the photograph shows a lens inspection, so
  only a clip-art balance proves the line. All six critics said so.
- **V1:**
  - about 14 text elements;
  - the lens hid four of the eight pearls, so "no two match" had to be proved by a drawn line-up;
  - the line-up also traced pearls the plate did not show.
- **V3:** the answer "Isiliye toh liya." was set as the smallest row, so the ad read as aunty's complaint.

**The change: one claim, proved by the picture, answered under the headline.** Every k01b card carries it:

1. **The drawn lens sits on exactly what the headline names.**

   The rest of the piece stays visible at true size (C1, C5, each card's layout).
2. **The buyer's answer sits directly under the headline at headline weight** (the comeback row, C4).
3. **Six text elements on the Feed** (C4). The FIG callouts, the seal and the acuity marks move to the Story.
4. **A fact the photograph cannot prove goes in the 6/6 line,** never in the headline.

**Zero-credit polish for k01-4,** as in review.md fix 1: V1's line "EK BHI MATCH NAHI KARTA." with "Isiliye toh liya."

## 8. Design lessons

**Which variation type won, and why.**

- **V2, the B type** (a pushed headline: the bible's balance-beam glyph made the call "BHAARI HAI. ACHHA HAI." on two
  short rows with one device), won by 0.04.
  - **Why it won:** the thumb test (5.92 against 5.25 and 4.75), stop (6.5) and copy (7.17), with the most quotable line
    in the camp.
  - **What it lost:** fit and message, because its device (a balance) proved what the photograph did not.
- **V1, the A type** (the bible as written: the line-up of eight traced pearls, a FIG callout, the quote, the stamp and the
  eye-chart numbers), had the better selling numbers:
  - would-buy 5.67 and buy-yes 0.67;
  - half the customer favourites.

  It lost on layout (5.33, about 14 text elements) and at 200 px, where its lower third turned to clutter.
- **V3, the C type** (proof-led: a cream eye-chart poster over a half-circle crop), lost on every count:
  - stop 5.33, desire 5.5, customers 5.17, buy-yes 0;
  - the poster ground shrank the bracelet to a sliver;
  - the Story ended in an empty plum block.

**What that means for k01b** (diff §7, the variation plan):

- **The template is V2's layout carrying V1's message:** two short rows, the answer under them, one device and the card.
- **Drawn devices must be hairline type tied to the photograph,** never clip art. Ananya called the brass balance
  "busy", and Meera called it "too much".
- **The piece needs more of the Feed.** At 200 px the bracelet read as "a few white blobs", so the drawn lens is at least
  30% of the Feed width (learnings 17).
- **The 2x lens on a 2k plate upsamples about 1.4x.** The k01r mocks show it holds at Feed size. Still check each crop
  against reference 1 (C6).
- **The trust line was the only compliance "fail",** and every critic marked it under the old rule. Paste the owner
  ruling into every critic's prompt (learnings 28).
