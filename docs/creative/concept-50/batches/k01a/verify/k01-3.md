**Verdict on construction: FAIL.** The element count and order hold, and the circlet is hollow and lopsided with the green stone on top. But the two four-point stars, the exact thing this card's headline and constellation overlay are about, are not stars, and the band is not polished. The camp's honest 2x lens would put both faults front and centre. This is the card's own named morph: "drop the 2 four-point stars or turn them into" something else.

It wears the ref 01 face-on version, with the big pearl toward her face. In the real worn photo (02, left ear), the big pearl sits toward the back of the head. So this is the other ear's stud. The stud is still a real member of the pair, so this is a note, not a fail.

## 3. Scale

- **Bounding box (src):** x 809-916 and y 1501-1652, so **107 x 151 px**.
- **Feed px per mm:** 151 x 0.703125 / 16.8 = **6.3 outside a lens**, and 12.6 inside a 2x lens. That is under the 19 line, so no "Shown magnified" line is triggered. The brief planned 19.6 in the lens, from an 11 cm field on a 4K plate. This plate is 2k, and its field is about 30 cm (head and shoulders), not 11 cm.
- **Referent (ear, same plane):**
  - In the take, the ear runs from the helix top at y 1257 to the lobe bottom at about y 1530, so it is about 273 px tall. The stud is 151 / 273 = **0.55 of the ear's height**.
  - In the real worn photo 02, the stud is 73 px on a 208 px ear, or **0.35** (`R02_ear_3x.png`).
  - So the take draws the stud **about 1.6x too big**. Against an average 57 mm adult ear it is about 1.9x.
  - The aunty's thumbnail (about 60-70 px) agrees: the scene runs about 4.6-5.8 px per mm, so a true 16.8 mm stud would be about 80-95 px tall, not 151.
- **Verdict:** this is **unintended mis-scale over 30%**. The camp's device is "about 2× in her lens, true size outside", and this stud is already about 1.6x outside any lens.
- **Size line as planned:** "IN HER LENS ABOUT 2× · ACTUAL 16.8 MM TALL". It states the listed size honestly, but on this plate the true-size promise outside the lens would not hold.
