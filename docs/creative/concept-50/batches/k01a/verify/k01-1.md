## 2. Colour

The braid's mean RGB is (191, 140, 83), against (203, 152, 82) on reference 03. That is a warm 18k gold tone, slightly darker and duller because of the satin finish. It is not brassy or orange, and no amber or plum cast misstates the metal. The discs read paler (champagne) than the braid.

The glass has a faint grey haze and a pale reflection streak at its lower right. This is minor: the HTML lens replaces the glass anyway, but the 2x crop will carry the haze.

## 3. Scale

- **Bounding box (src):** x 658-935, y 1308-1619. That is 278 px wide, and 312 px tall from the latch tops (about 295 px from the disc tops).
- **Feed px per mm:** 278 x 0.703125 / 25 = **7.8 outside the lens**. At 2x inside the lens it is **15.6**, under the 19 line and short of the brief's planned 19.6. The brief planned a 4K plate with an 11 cm field. This plate is 2k, and the field is about 19 cm.
- **Referent (ear):** the ear runs from the helix top at y 868 to the lobe tip at y 1336, so it is 468 px tall. The hoop is 278 / 468 = **0.59 of the ear's height**. A true 25 mm hoop on an adult ear of about 57-60 mm would be about 0.42-0.44 of it, or about 205 px. Even the card's looser "about half the ear's height" gives 234 px. **The hoop is drawn about 1.2-1.4x, most likely about 1.35x**, which is the known drift. The camp's device depends on "outside the lens the piece stays true size" ("About 2× in her lens, true size outside"), so this mis-scale is a fault, not scale play.
- **Size line as planned:** "IN HER LENS ABOUT 2× · ACTUAL 25 MM ACROSS". It is honest about the lens and the listed size, but on this plate the true-size claim outside the lens would not hold. No "Shown magnified" line is triggered, because the plate is under 19 px per mm both outside and inside the lens.
