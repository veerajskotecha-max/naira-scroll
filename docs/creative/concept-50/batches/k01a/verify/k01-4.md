## Verdict: FAIL, fixable by one regeneration. Choice: none.

The bracelet itself is faithful: the toggle, the drop on its jump ring, the beads and the mismatched baroque pearls all match reference 1, and the scale against the wrist matches reference 2. The fail is the camp's device and the grid:

1. **The lens is not over the piece.** The glass hangs about 130 src px (about 18 mm at the wrist, 93 Feed px) **above** the toggle. It shows only the edge of the forearm and darkness. An honest 2x fill about the lens centre would therefore magnify bare skin and black (see `A_lens_2x_fill_preview.png`), and "IN HER LENS ABOUT 2×" cannot be printed.
2. **The glasses are the wrong frame.**
   - They are cat-eye, full-rim frames, not the camp's half-moons (the seal glyph is two flat-topped D shapes).
   - Only one lens of the pair is visible.
   - The glass is tinted and hazy.
3. **The grid is broken.**
   - The bracelet sits at 47-58% of the frame's height, below the planned 33-53%, so the Story's compact card covers the lower pearls.
   - The Feed line-up band sits on the bracelet.

This is the same fault class as k01-2: the lens is off the piece and the frames are not half-moons. Here it misses by about 18 mm, not by 550 Feed px.
