Take A is the picture this ad wants. It shows the real clasp, front on and symmetric, and her own half-moon lens frames the sailor ring like a target. At 200 px it is the strongest frame of the batch.

It fails on one measurable fault: the necklace is drawn about one and a half times too big for the woman wearing it:
- the neck is only 3.5 ring-widths wide;
- her fingers measure about 10 mm at the ring's scale.

The camp's whole device is "about 2× in her lens, true size outside", and the ad will print "RING ACTUAL 22 MM" beside a ring that reads about 32 mm on her. That is the unintended mis-scale over 30% that the rules name, and the same fault that failed k01-3.

Take B fixed the scale (its counts against body landmarks worked), but it broke the piece's colour:
- a white glare sits on the glass;
- the bar's jump ring and the last link render in silver tone;
- the frames are round, not half-moons;
- the clasp sits too low for the Story card.
