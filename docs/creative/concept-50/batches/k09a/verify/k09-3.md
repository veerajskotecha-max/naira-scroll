## 5. Idea and stop power

One narrow blade of shop light finds the ring alone, and the walls stay dark. Why it scores: "the moment every family knows, when the safe opens and the light finds the one green-and-white piece."

- **A: crazy 5, stop200 7.** The door is exactly the brief's: a thick brushed edge, three polished bolts, open a hand's width on a narrow cool gap. At 200 px (`AB_feed_200`), the bolts read "locker" before anything else, and then the large green-and-white ring fills the dark interior.
  - Missing: no visible blade rakes the ring, because the front fill lights it evenly. The camera reads as looking into the opened safe more than out of it.
  - The family-locker idea and the BHAAV NAHI KHAATI joke land, and the piece is the loudest colour in the frame.
- **B: crazy 6, stop200 5.** The point of view is the more unusual one: the frame of the safe, the slab of the door, and a bright shop beyond.
  - But the door stands wide open, not a hand's width, and the bolts are dim and soft.
  - The bright doorway is the strongest value in the frame and pulls the eye off a ring that is only 38% of the width.
  - At 200 px it reads as a ring on a block in a dark room; the safe read arrives late. It is a fine product still that loses the instant locker read.
- **Both** are tasteful hero stills more than wild ideas. The craziness of this card lives in the line and the tag strings, as planned.
