## Verdict: A FAILS, B PASSES WITH NOTES. Choice: B

- **Take B** is faithful. Its clasp matches ref 2 part for part:
  - the flat gate end at 11 o'clock and the thin gate across the top;
  - a plain barrel at 1 o'clock, with the right strand's jump ring round the tube;
  - the nub and the grooves on the right side;
  - a straight bar with one row of about 9 tiny stones on its jump ring.

  The metal tone matches ref 1. The '20%' device fits at full ring height in Feed, and the Story lands on the plan's own numbers. Its one deviation: the bar did not slide back as B's prompt asked. It lies across the ring's upper-left tube, exactly as in ref 2. That is faithful to the piece, and the 0 still reads (see 5).
- **Take A** redesigns the bar. It has **5 large prong-set stones** (each about 1.7 mm, on a 2.8 mm pitch) where both references show about 9 tiny pave stones in one row. A secondary drift: a **coil-ribbed barrel at 1 o'clock** plus a banded sleeve at 3-4 o'clock. The reference's barrel is plain, so A has ribs in two places, the same "doubled collar" drift that failed k04-2 A. A is also the more saturated, CG-looking take: its metal saturation is 0.66 against ref 1's 0.46, and its velvet is a rust red instead of cocoa. One regeneration could fix A, but B already passes, so no regeneration is needed.
