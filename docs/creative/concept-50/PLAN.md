# Concept 50: production plan (10 camps x 5 ads, aiming at ROAS 5)

Naira Petite (nairaflore.com) · plan written 20 Nov 2026 · nothing generated yet · machine-readable twin: `plan.json`

This plan is the brief for 10 batches of 5. Each batch is generated, verified, designed in three variations, rated blind by experts and customers, and closed with a loop note that must change the next batch's briefs before it runs ("redo the next 5 better"). RULINGS.md wins wherever anything here disagrees with it.

## 1. The money: from 2.2-2.9 to 5

- **Where we are.** Catalogue carousels are the account's engine (figures in the local Meta notes, not committed). The best single creative is F2 (ruby necklace worn lips-to-chest, 4.74x; PLAYBOOK §1). Recent good campaigns sit at 2.2-2.9. Creator reels, clean-lifestyle statics and hand-built lookbook carousels lost money: when the concept, prop or body is the hero, she clicks and does not buy.
- **Why 2.2 does not become 5 on its own.** Most orders are one piece (about ₹1,150 after a code). ROAS 5 on one piece needs a cost per purchase of about ₹230, lower than the account has ever reached.
- **The plan's arithmetic.** ROAS 5 does not need a cheaper purchase than our best; it needs catalogue-level cost per purchase on a bigger basket. The three-piece baskets named in this plan come to ₹2,118-3,598 after 20% off (the cheapest possible trio, two ₹699 pieces and one at ₹899, is ₹1,838), so at ₹480 a purchase they return 4.4-7.5x.
- **The target pair every batch is judged against.** If 40% of a camp's orders are 3 pieces (about ₹2,400), 20% are 2 (about ₹1,800) and 40% are 1 (about ₹1,100), the order value is about ₹1,760 and ROAS 5 needs about ₹350 a purchase. At 50% three-piece orders (and 30% singles) the order value is about ₹1,890 and ROAS 5 needs about ₹380. So every camp carries both levers: a thumb-stop that keeps the cost at catalogue level, and a card 5 plus primary text that make three the obvious order.
- **How each camp is built for that.** Every camp behaves like the catalogue that earns the money: one large, bright, named and priced piece per card (the frame that won the blind 200 px read), each card linked to its own product page, a recognisable series look, card 5 = BUY3 (BUY4 in c04) linked to a collection, and every single ad's primary text carrying the camp's BUY3 line. Each concept gives a human reason for three (two to gift and one to keep; one piece per function; three gifts on his list; the haggle she wins). The order value is the idea, never a discount poster: no sums, no compare-at, no red SALE, the code's value in the F2 box.
- **Owner option that lowers friction (no store change made here):** a Shopify discount link (`/discount/BUY3?redirect=/collections/...`) on card 5 applies the code in the bag; the offer box then drops the word CODE (PLAYBOOK §4.0).

## 7. The loop: redo the next 5 better

**Rule.** Redo the next 5 better: no batch starts until the previous batch's loop note has changed its briefs (prompt clauses, copy, layout, product or order), and the change is logged as a diff. 'No change' is allowed only with a reason.

**Expert panel:** Performance buyer (ROAS, cost per purchase, purchase cues, basket logic); Craft creative director (stop power, layout, one message, brand series, award-level wit); Production and truth verifier (fidelity, scale, casts, compliance).

**Customer panel:** Meera, 38, Pune: three December weddings; the core buyer (women 35-44); Sunita, 48, Lucknow: mother of a groom this season; women 45-54; Ananya, 29, Bengaluru: design-literate, office Secret Santa; women 25-34; Rohan, 22, Delhi: student, buys gifts for maa, didi and his girlfriend; men 18-24.

**Ratings.** Each panellist rates every finished ad 1-10 (experts: §7 weighted + 200 px read; customers: stop, want, trust, would-buy yes/no and the 3 pieces they would buy) and gives one line. The batch's expert mean, customer mean and basket rate (share of customer answers naming 3+ pieces) are recorded.

**Every loop note records:** scores (expert mean, customer mean, per ad, basket rate); fix target: the lowest §7 dimension across the batch, with the change that fixes it in the next batch; one new move tried in the next batch; keep: what worked and is carried forward (named device, prompt clause, layout); drop: what failed and is banned for the rest of the run; brief diff: the exact edits to the next batch's five briefs; verification lessons: every redo's cause and the prompt clause that fixed it; credits spent and balance; stock re-read.

**Thresholds and triggers:**
- Batch pass: expert mean ≥ 7.8 AND customer mean ≥ 7.8 AND at least 4 of 5 ads ship.
- Basket check: if fewer than half of the customer answers name 3 pieces, the next batch strengthens card 5 and the primary text (bigger offer box, a named trio, 'two to gift, one to keep' logic).
- If a batch's customer mean is more than 0.5 below its expert mean, the next batch adds desire (warmer light, an occasion in the picture, a worn or hand cue) without losing the 200 px read.
- If a batch's expert 200 px read loses to the loser anchor on 2+ ads, the next batch enlarges the piece (to the upper end of its share band) and calms the ground.
