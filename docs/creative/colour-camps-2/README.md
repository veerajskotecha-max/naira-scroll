# Colour Camps 2.0 — the quiet edition

A classier, minimal redo of the APRICOT, SAGE and LILAC ads, built on the same fifteen photographs that were verified
against their listings for version 1 (`../colour-camps/`). Built 28 Sep 2026.

Artifact (both directions side by side, with a before/after and save buttons): https://claude.ai/artifact/TuHn6wh5seMCV9GL7Dz9Yv

| Folder | Holds |
|---|---|
| `framed/` | **Recommended.** Each photograph on a mat in its camp colour, with name, italic line and price beneath. 15 story ads (1080×1920), 15 feed ads (1080×1350) and 32 carousel cards |
| `quiet/` | Full-bleed photographs with only the wordmark, name and price. Same set of 62 files |
| `build/` | The layout system (`minimal.py`), the set modules, the export and the artifact page |

Card 1 of each carousel is that product's feed ad; the files named `carousel-2…4` follow it.

## Why 1.0 felt loud

- **Ten or more elements per frame:** logo, header line, italic line, giant camp word, coloured dot, device, name,
  spec line, price and button.
- **Four typefaces:** Velista, Cormorant, Jost and a monospace. The monospace reads as software, not jewellery.
- **The camp word was the biggest thing on every ad.** It was bigger than the piece and partly hidden behind it,
  so "SAGE" read as "SAGF".
- **Stickers, paint chips, stamps and postmarks read as bargain or novelty,** and the price sticker repeated the
  price printed at the foot.
- **A Shop button inside the image duplicated Meta's own button.** On Stories, both the button and the top header sat
  where the platform's overlays cover them.
- **Green ink on apricot was a complementary clash,** and a pale gradient behind the text washed out the photographs.

## The 2.0 rules

1. **The photograph is the ad.** It is shown exactly as verified, with no stickers, chips, stamps, giant word or
   button.
2. **Three pieces of text at most:** the wordmark, the product name and the price. The framed direction adds the
   listing's own opening line in italic, such as "Texture does the talking here."
3. **The website's own type:** Velista capitals for names, Jost tracked capitals for prices and labels, and
   Cormorant italic for the single line.
4. **Tone-on-tone ink:** cocoa on apricot, deep green on sage, aubergine on lilac. Each camp also gets one fixed mat
   colour (#EFD7C8, #D9E6DE and #E2D6E6).
5. **Stories keep the top 250 px and the bottom reply-bar zone clear of all text.**
6. **Carousel cards carry one verified fact each,** in the listing's words ("Worn · about 25 mm across"). The last
   card carries the name and price. There are no page counters, because Meta shows its own dots.
7. **On full-bleed, the caption never crosses the piece.** It moves above the piece when there is no room below.
   Where it falls on texture, a feathered haze (a soft blur and a faint wash of the local colour) keeps it readable
   without a visible box.

## Choosing

**Framed** reads as one campaign, keeps the colour camps through the mats, and always sets the words on a clean
ground. **Full-bleed** shows the photograph at its largest and is the more immersive of the two, especially in
Stories. Both use the same photographs, prices and stock as version 1: Shopify on 27 Sep 2026, all fifteen pieces
active.
