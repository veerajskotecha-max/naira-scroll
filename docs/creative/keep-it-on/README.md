## Why this campaign exists

Read across the earlier volumes — The Long Afternoon, The Bench, Pigment, Sugarplay —
and the same three holes appear in all of them:

| Gap | What every prior volume did | What the catalogue actually says |
|---|---|---|
| **One piece per frame** | Single hero, isolated | The pricing is basket-shaped. Nothing showed the stack. |
| **Dry** | Studio-dry, always | **46 of 50 active SKUs** carry *"waterproof and tarnish free — shower, swim, sweat"* in Care. Never once shown. |
| **Still** | Object at rest | Nothing showed the piece mid-life. |

Volume Three attacks all three at once. The waterproof claim is written into the listings
and has never been photographed; that is the single largest unclaimed asset in the catalogue.

## The rule that breaks a rule

Every prior volume held **macro + body fragments only, no full body**. This one deliberately
uses the hand, wrist, neck and ear as the *primary* subject.

That is justified, not an exception for its own sake: the body is the most trustworthy scale
referent we have measured. It sits at the top of the hierarchy —

> body > product's own imprint > tool with an engraved scale > printed grid (worst)

so a campaign built on bodies solves the exact size-compression problem the no-body rule was
protecting against.

## The loop record

Five frames failed verification and were re-shot. Each failure is the same lesson in a
different costume: **name the property, not the label.**

| Plate | What failed | The fix |
|---|---|---|
| 02 Mirror | Too much face; the wiped-arc device didn't read | Re-framed to the ear alone, swipe named as a curved band through dense condensation |
| 05 Pool | Hand half-dry, refraction stayed polite. Second attempt rendered the green stones round, not oval | Whole hand under the surface; named the physics — the underside of the water as a wrinkled mirror throwing the hand back down, bubbles in the pavé, cyan shift, caustic net. Round-stone frame discarded |
| 06 Hair | Water arc rendered as glass/crystal beads | "Hundreds of irregular water beads frozen by a fast shutter", with *no glass, no crystal* in negatives |
| 07 Sweat | Chain far too heavy — spec is 4mm, about a matchstick | Restated as fine and delicate, toggle pinned at ~4× chain width |
| 08 Bath | Twice. Second pass came back a product still-life on green velvet, no arm, no water | The reference photo is shot on green velvet and I had dropped the *"ignore reference 1's background"* clause. Restoring it, plus "this is on a human arm and is not a product still-life", fixed it |

## Honest limits

- Plate 07 shows more of the face than the neck-down standard allows. Usable; tighter is better.
- Four more plates are planned and unbuilt: face-wash in cupped hands, sea and salt,
  tea steam, and one dry morning-rhythm break to keep the set from becoming monotone.
