# Naira Petite: static playbook for 500 Meta ads

*This is a read-only study dated 19 Nov 2026. Nothing was generated, written to Shopify or changed in Meta. Board ranks refer to the winners board (artifact 4QL6qL6EkKzrRwxiUsdb4f). Our own numbers come from Meta account 903466039148509, pulled on 19 Nov 2026, unless a different date is given. The study was checked against the kit in `/home/user/naira-scroll/docs/creative/ads-500/kit/` (kit.css tokens, the Velista, Cormorant, Jost, Bricolage and Anton fonts, and the four logo PNGs) and against the plates named in §6.*

---

## 0. The decision in one screen

1. **Our statics underperform, but two prove the bar can be cleared.** Statics returned 1.02x (₹96,261 spend, 65 purchases, 20 Oct to 18 Nov). Catalogue carousels returned 2.28x. Two statics did much better:
   - **Twenty F2:** a worn crop, ₹2,348 spend, 6 sales, 4.74x.
   - **Ruby anaar:** a Story with no text, ₹14,248 spend, 24 sales, 2.29x.

   Both follow the same grammar: one piece shown large, one saturated colour field that rhymes with it, little or no text, and the name, price and offer in the lower third.
2. **The winners board adds moves we have never made:**
   - a graphic layer that makes the claim literal (ranks 1, 40, 55);
   - monument scale with an honest size tag (26, 42, 95, 190);
   - two-beat headlines (4, 26, 55, 57);
   - giant numerals taken from the listing (11, 22, 88);
   - set mechanics (5, 25, 58);
   - same-body evidence (20, 21, 92).
3. **The system** has 14 archetypes and one fixed foot lockup, and builds Feed 4:5 first. Its rules:
   - the piece takes at least 35% of the canvas width;
   - no more than 4 text elements;
   - a verified offer on every ad;
   - no water imagery.
4. **The volume plan** is 100 batches of 5: 466 single-product ads plus 34 set ads, across the 18 SKUs that are live and have 20 or more units. It uses 95 new Higgsfield frames (190 credits), about 60 re-verified existing plates, and cut-outs of listing packshots.
5. **Five pre-flight checks come before batch 1:**
   - confirm the live offer terms;
   - add the missing ₹ glyph to the kit fonts;
   - confirm the Toggle clasp reference;
   - hold Triple Coil until its turn count is confirmed;
   - re-read stock and prices.
6. **Building 500 is not the same as running 500.** In the last 30 days, 169 statics averaged about ₹570 of spend each, and 163 ads took ₹50,386 without a single sale. Launch in waves with enough spend per ad (§5.6).

## 1. What winners do

### 1.1 Visual
- **One claim, made literal with one prop or setting:**
  - a sun arc for duration (1);
  - a spotlight for evening (3);
  - a light burst for boldness (4);
  - a ladder for steps (28);
  - a pour for texture (52);
  - a neon window (56);
  - a glass shield for protection (87);
  - ice for cool (100);
  - a cocktail glass for a product named 'cocktail' (19);
  - a padlock-shaped pack for 'locked in' (82).
- **The product shown with the mark it leaves:** swatch (67), lash stroke (77), mist (66), drip (108), stripes (120), the pencil drawing through the headline (55, 81).
- **One colour world per frame, of two kinds:**
  - tone-on-tone pulled from the pack: 22, 28, 52, 53, 54, 9, 19, 35, 74, 100, 110, 127, 135, 153, 190, 195;
  - one saturated flat field: 17, 26, 42, 34, 76, 118.
- **The product made monumental,** tilted and cropped off one or two edges: 26, 42, 57, 66, 95, 175, 187.
- **The result as the hero, with no product in frame:** 14, 45, 105, 32, 198, 40, 169, 194.
- **Face and product read as one unit:** 70, 88, 89, 126, 143, 162, 177, 179.
- **The same subject twice, with one thing changed:** 20, 21, 39, 75, 92, 150, 178, 181.
- **What does not transfer:** the oversized bottle laid on an arm (22) and the forced-perspective push toward the lens (24). For jewellery these would be a scale lie.

### 1.2 Camera and light
- **Product-only CGI or packshots dominate** (15 of 19 in the benefit 1/3 group). The product fills 45 to 90% of the frame height, is tilted 5 to 40 degrees and is cropped by the frame (1, 26, 42, 52, 95, 122).
- **Hard key light or backlight** gives a glint or bloom, and low hero angles make the product feel monumental (1, 3, 15, 175, 187). Further variants:
  - spotlight on black: 3, 166;
  - hard window sun with patterned shadows: 72, 83, 91, 123;
  - raking chiaroscuro: 124, 106.
- **People are shot at 70 to 100 mm, eye level, chest-up, with the face cropped.** The product is held at the face plane, at true scale in the hand (2, 18, 70, 89, 126, 162). Body fragments always serve a demonstration (11, 22, 40, 86).
- **Evidence frames are locked off,** with identical crop and exposure (20, 21, 39, 75, 178). Macro texture becomes the whole picture (106, 146, 190, 199).

### 1.3 Layout
- **A hard two-zone split** puts the product on one side and the text on the other. This is about 12 of 19 in the benefit 3/3 group, and also 11, 22, 57, 66, 68, 77, 87, 107, 108.
- **The headline is the largest type,** unless a number is (11, 22, 88, 169).
- **Text covers 10 to 40% of the frame.** The strongest frames sit at 8 to 30%: 6 at 8%, 53 at 12%, 67 at 14%, and 76 has tape only.
- **Support lines are hairline leader callouts** (40, 55, 79, 29, 183, 186) or numbered or verb triads (22, 28, 52, 66).
- **Headlines often use two weights:** a light set-up line, then a heavy pay-off line (68, 77, 96, and at least 8 of 16 in the poster group).
- **A stat system sits bottom-left:** a giant thin numeral, a 2 to 4 word label at cap height, and a footnote (2, 7, 41, 113, 119, 21).
- **Almost none carry a CTA button or a price.** The exceptions are the savings band (24), the offer line (5) and the offer ribbon (176).

### 1.4 Branding
- **The pack carries the logo; there are almost no corner logos.** One accent colour is lifted from the pack into a headline keyword or a proof chip: Foxtale orange (22, 28, 52), Sugar pink (4, 40, 55), BSC blue (26, 42), mCaffeine blue (11).
- **Brands run a repeatable series system:**
  - BSC 26 and 42 share a face and a blue;
  - Foxtale 53 and 54 use the same right-side text slot;
  - Arata 2, 7, 41, 113, 119 and 124 share cream condensed numerals, a rotated 'Up to' and a tiled set;
  - Dr. Sheth's 14, 45 and 105 use a mirrored template;
  - Sugar 5, 62 and 80 share a glitter serif and sun stripes;
  - Pilgrim 157, 180 and 187 share a teal serif, a seal and a footnote.
- **Type pairing:** one display face plus one sans, with the second beat switching to italic or the accent colour (4, 40, 55). Rank 107 adds a constant sign-off band.
- **For Naira:** jewellery has no pack to carry a logo, so the canvas has to carry a fixed wordmark (107).

### 1.5 Copy
- **Two-beat lines:**
  - 'Bold Lashes. Zero Smudge.' (4);
  - 'Stays put. Steals attention.' (55);
  - 'No drama. Just freshness.' (26);
  - 'Tough on hair / gentle on your skin' (17);
  - 'Looks like a pillow. Feels like a hug.' (79);
  - 'Light on weight / big on journeys' (96).
- **Number first:** 72 Hrs (11), 32% (22), 90 Mins (31), 17% (88), 48 HOURS (162), 63.3% (185), 40% = … (169).
- **Other devices:**
  - verb triads (22, 28, 52, 66);
  - personification: 'The chill one.' (42), notes that 'bring the island's juiciest gossip' (101);
  - simile: 'Glow like you just left a Spa' (27), 'glides like butter' (199);
  - pun (47, 82, 142, 155);
  - question and answer: 'WHAT IS IT?' (109), 'WHAT GOES INSIDE?' (195);
  - self-identification lists (111, 167);
  - occasion or time framing (39, 71, 159, 161);
  - Hinglish: 'DE-TAN HAR ROZ' (152);
  - an idiom made literal (127);
  - familiarity plus news: 'Your Fav…, Now Upgraded!' (186).
- **Length:** headlines run 2 to 11 words, most of them 3 to 7.

### 1.6 Offer and proof
- **Proof sits right beside the claim** as a chip, disc, seal, card or footnote: 1, 11, 57, 77, 99, 138, 157, 187.
- **Small samples are disclosed openly:** n=15 (14, 45), n=30 (11), n=35 (21, 61), n=50 (46, 154).
- **Review cards carry a first name and a city:** 13, 81, 140, 156.
- **Comparisons show the brand in colour and the rival in grey:** 8, 30, 37, 73, 102, 158.
- **The offer is rarely printed on the canvas.** Named packs carry it instead (5, 25, 58, 59, 139).
- **Implications for Naira:**
  - We have no verified review, rating or customer count, so we cannot borrow the proof layer. Listing specs and service facts (COD, free insured shipping) have to do that job.
  - Our own winners all carry an offer (§2), so we keep it even though the board mostly leaves it off.

## 3. What is missing from our creatives, ranked by expected impact

**1. The piece is not the hero, so the thumb does not stop.**
- **Our evidence:**
  - Jewellery takes 2 to 5% of the frame in performance/ads/crazy3/06-woven-gold-hoops-night-feed.jpg, crazy3/21-brushed-gold-huggies-day-story.jpg, colour/15-ribbon-bead-bracelet-feed.jpg, colour/14 and colour/67.
  - The ring is about 3% of the frame in diwali-10/ads/09; the mesh ring is about 12% in finished-ads/e2/out/E2_chai_a1.jpg; the bracelet is hidden under a stamp in night-shift/02-the-test/feed/T5-perfume.
  - Text panels take 40 to 50% of night-shift and three-camps cards.
  - In the Meta data the losers were 'concept outranks product': lanterns, gummies, a ruler, a nutrition label.
- **Winner evidence:** the product is 45 to 90% of the frame height and cropped by the frame (26, 42, 57, 95, 175). Our own best static, F2, has the piece large and sharp.
- **Fix:** the piece takes at least 35% of the canvas width (at least 60% for macro archetypes), and no text ever touches it. A prop gets no more visual weight than the piece.

**2. There is no offer or value layer on the image.**
- **Our evidence:**
  - NAIRA10, BUY4, COD and free insured shipping never appear on any image in about 400 files (audit 2: zero hits in the build sources).
  - The E2, diwali-10, colour and crazy3 sets carry no offer at all. night-shift/07-the-light argues against offers ('NO CODE. NO TIMER. NO SALE.').
  - Every Meta winner carries BUY2/BUY3 or NAIRA10, and Core v2 with NAIRA10 is the account's best catalogue at 3.00x.
- **Winner evidence:** an offer band with live terms (24), a ₹0 numeric antithesis (136), a disc straddling the panel edge (57), a named edit plus offer line (5), a named pack (25, 58).
- **Fix:** every ad carries one verified offer chip in the foot lockup. One archetype (A11) leads with the offer.

**3. Worn photographs are rare, generic, and sometimes the wrong size.**
- **Our evidence:**
  - The only worn static that ran is the best static (F2, 4.74x).
  - The 26 worn performance ads all use the same ivory shirt and low bun (audit 1, sheet s6_worn.jpg).
  - Scale has failed: performance/ads/lilac-papillon-ring-worn-feed.jpg spans about 32 mm against a true 20 mm, and the Soleil pendant came out at 32 to 34 mm against 18 mm.
  - No full frame has a person in context.
- **Winner evidence:** 24 of 200 board ads are 'face, product and one line' (70, 88, 89, 126, 148, 162), plus body macros with callouts (40, 141).
- **Fix:** A2 and A13 are generated directly at true size against a named body referent, on dark saturated grounds. Frames that fail scale are discarded, never edited.

**4. Product fidelity has to be fixed before scaling, starting with the Toggle (77 units).**
- **Our evidence:**
  - The Toggle clasp is drawn as a plain donut with a long bar on about 20 cards: night-shift/03-the-clasp/feed/K1-toggle (its callout reads 'BAR · ABOUT 15MM'), A3-gold, all of 06-on-every-skin, G2-chain, M3-one-neck, red-room R4-pour, keep-it-on/07-sweat.
  - worlds-and-hours/feed/C5-link shows no bar at all.
  - concept-camps/build/sheet_lines.json logs 8 or more failed Toggle renders.
  - The Brushed Huggies locks still say 12 mm; the live size is 14 × 9.8 mm.
- **Winner evidence:** proof sits right on the visible part (60's dashed rings, 145's dimension lines, 79's cutaway).
- **Fix:**
  - Lock the clasp to concept-camps/build/refs/toggle/ref-clasp.jpg and the listing worn photo.
  - Re-measure every reused plate against the 19 Nov truth sheet.
  - Prefer existing listing photos wherever a render keeps failing.

**5. Headlines name concepts instead of giving reasons to buy.**
- **Our evidence:**
  - Concept-noun headlines: 'APRICOT.', 'LILAC.', 'SAGE.', 'THE CALLIPER.', '#C48B5F.' (colour-camps, night-shift/08), 'QUIET.' (worlds C4).
  - Generic occasion lines stayed under 2.2% CTR. ads-20 S1_01 to S1_05 have no headline at all.
  - The proven 'X, not Y' line (5.81 to 7.25% CTR) gets shrunk into a lowercase correction.
  - The correction formula '[colour] cubic zirconia, not X' is worn out (finished-ads/review_r24.json).
- **Winner evidence:** two-beat lines (4, 26, 55, 57, 79), number first (11, 22, 88), personification (42), simile (27).
- **Fix:** the headline states the craft truth, the benefit or the mechanism in 2 to 6 words. The 'X, not Y' formula moves up to headline size.

**6. Camera and light all look the same.**
- **Our evidence:**
  - A 30 to 45 degree tabletop, soft window light from the upper left and cream plaster across about 280 concepts (audit 1).
  - 16 of 21 E2 feed ads sit on a pale neutral ground.
  - The pale pastels lost: Prism S5 (₹2,441, 0 sales), Heartbead S1 (₹1,540, 0 sales). The dark, saturated frames won: F2 on cocoa, W1 on oxblood, ruby on red.
- **Winner evidence:**
  - spotlight on black (3);
  - hard sun with patterned shadows (72, 91, 123);
  - low hero angles (1, 15, 175);
  - a saturated flat field (17, 26, 118);
  - macro texture walls (106, 190).
- **Fix:** every archetype carries its own camera and light spec (§4). Grounds are one saturated hue or a deep dark. Pale grounds only behind a piece with strong contrast.

**7. Most archetypes are missing.**
- **Our evidence:** there are no anatomy callouts on a large macro (X07 was cut because its marks landed on the metal), no equation, no before/after, no comparison, no type-led flat poster, no how-to or size card beyond ten-creatives/feed/A2-actual-size, no proof, and no styling decoder.
- **Winner evidence:**
  - callouts and equations: 38 of 200 (40, 79, 29, 48, 169);
  - before/after: 21 of 200 (20, 21, 92);
  - us-versus-them: 10 of 200 (8, 30, 37);
  - decoders (50, 36, 51).
- **Reconciling this with our losers:** our infographic ads (A07, P2, SA08) lost because the product was small and the text was heavy. The winners put callouts on a product that fills the frame, at 12 to 20% text (40, 79). Callouts are allowed only on top of a monument-size piece.

**8. There are no bundles, and stacks are priced at the full sum.**
- **Our evidence:**
  - night-shift/05-the-layer reads 'THE STACK ₹2,998'; worlds-and-hours/story/D5 reads '₹7,794'.
  - BUY2 and BUY3 appear only in primary text, and BUY4 never appears.
  - The repo README calls basket size the lever, yet only 5 of more than 75 ads are bundles.
- **Winner evidence:** an occasion map (25), an equation (58), a step staircase (59), a named edit (5), 'what's in the box' (16, 103).
- **Fix:** A10 is a set builder with a BUY code as the hook and no price sums. Every piece in a set must have 20 or more units.

**9. There is no single brand system.**
- **Our evidence:**
  - About eight visual systems are in use (audit 2, W11).
  - The wordmark is 105 to 170 px wide and moves between top-left, bottom-right and centre. It is missing on ads-20/ads/S2_08 and lost on a white shirt in night-shift/04/M3.
  - None of the kit fonts has the ₹ sign (it falls back to Inter).
  - Velista draws a 'TS' swash at letter-spacing 0.
- **Winner evidence:** series templates (Arata 2/7/41/119, Dr. Sheth's 14/45/105, BSC 26/42) and a constant sign-off (107).
- **Fix:** one foot lockup in the same place on every ad, one type ladder, and one ground hue per ad (§4.0).

**10. Stories are built badly.**
- **Our evidence:**
  - E2 Stories are 4:5 plates mirror-extended to 9:16 (E2_duo_a1-story has a mirrored floor; E2_tubogas has a dead bottom third).
  - The name, price and CTA sit under the reply bar on night-shift/10/story/A1 and APRICOT-A1-story.
  - Meta data: Story statics win only with the no-text ruby plate.
- **Fix:** the Story twin is the native 9:16 plate with no text, or with the wordmark only (A1, A3, A5). There are no stretched plates.

**11. There is no proof, and we cannot fabricate any.**
- **Our evidence:** zero reviews, ratings or counts in any file, and no review app installed (brand-system study).
- **Winner evidence:** 20 of 200 board ads lead with proof (13, 14, 81, 141).
- **Fix:** listing figures as stats (A12) and service facts as seals now. Verified reviews only after the owner decision in §8.

**12. There is no learning loop, and spend is spread too thin.**
- **Our evidence:**
  - All 15 night-shift and three-camps sets score themselves 9.0 to 9.5 (notes.json).
  - worlds-and-hours/README.md says nothing there was ever spend-tested.
  - 169 statics averaged about ₹570 of spend each.
- **Fix:** the batch critic loop (§5.3) and launch waves (§5.6).

**13. Files on record break the house rules.**
- **Our evidence:**
  - Banned water and colour-hold words: ten-creatives/feed/A1, A3, A4, A5; night-shift/02 (all five) and 10/A1 to A3; red-room R1, R2, R4; keep-it-on (the whole premise); finished-ads/ads.json P09.
  - A banned word in lilac-papillon-ring-crazy13-feed.jpg, -story, build/meta.csv lines 806 and 809, and copy.json line 2991.
  - 'RUBY' in the spec line of diwali-10/ads/01.
  - Price drift of up to ₹300 for the same SKU across sets.
- **Fix:** never reuse a finished ad. Reuse only text-free plates, after re-checking them.

## 4. The Naira static system

### 4.0 Rules that apply to every archetype

**Canvas and safe area**
- Feed master: 1080 × 1350.
- Text safe area: x 72 to 1008, y 84 to 1266.
- Story twin (9:16, only where an archetype allows one): text only between y 270 and 1500.

**Picture rules**
- The hero piece takes at least 35% of the canvas width.
- Never put text, a logo or a leader end on stones or metal.
- Gold renders warm-neutral, never brassy.
- Use one warm directional key plus one specular kick, with a true contact shadow.
- No AI star glints, floating objects or pasted shadows.
- No water, droplets, splash, shower, pool or wet skin anywhere (see §8).

**Size honesty**
- Every product-only macro carries a tag set in Jost 500, 22–24 px, caps, tracked .16em, inside a 2 px hairline box: 'SHOWN MAGNIFIED · ACTUAL 25 MM', using the listed figure.
- Worn frames are generated in Higgsfield at true size against a named body referent. They are cropped or scaled only as a whole frame, never with the piece changed relative to the body.

**Type ladder (px at 1080 wide)**

| Role | Face and size |
|---|---|
| H1 | Velista caps 84–120, tracked .02em (this also stops the TS swash), or Bricolage 800 88–130. Anton 140–220 on posters only. |
| Second beat | Cormorant Garamond italic 500, 0.55–0.9× H1, in the accent colour |
| Spec, callouts, body | Jost 400/500 26–30. Nothing smaller than 26 px except legal microtext and size tags, which may go to 22. |
| Name | Jost 500 26 caps, tracked .18em |
| Price | Jost 500 44 (at least 1.6× the name) |
| Giant numeral | Jost 300 240–320 |

- At most 4 text elements: the headline with both beats counts as one, a callout set counts as one, and the foot counts as one.
- Default text share is 20% or less; posters may go to 40%.

**The Naira foot (fixed on every ad: the series signature, after 107)**
