== 4. WHAT OUR WINNERS SHARE ==
- **Format:** catalogue carousels win at scale (2.0–3.0x). They show many in-stock pieces and let the shopper pick, and Meta writes name and price on every card.
  - The catalogue images are simple branded packshots: the piece on sage linen or lilac paper with one leaf or lavender sprig, small NAIRA wordmark at the top, square.
  - Video is second (Halo ring on Reels, Prism on Feed).
  - Statics win only when one idea lands hard.
- **Visual:** one piece, large and sharp; the jewellery fills about 40–60% of the frame width; true detail on the stones and claws.
  - The frame has one dominant colour field that rhymes exactly with the piece: ruby with pomegranate, pastel stones with pastel powder, gold with an oxblood gloss, gold with apricot.
  - Saturated, single-hue palettes (ruby red, oxblood, cocoa) beat busy pastel sets.
- **Camera:** close, low-angle or 45° macro, with shallow depth and the piece in the lower-middle of the frame. Light is warm and directional, with a real shadow and one specular hit.
  - The one worn winner (Twenty F2, 4.74x) is a tight editorial crop on warm skin against a dark backdrop, with a hand gesture.
  - Note: it shows the lips, which the older shoot brief banned.
- **Layout:** zero or minimal text on the image. The ruby anaar had none.
  - When there is text: wordmark on top, then one short headline (sensory or material, e.g. "WOVEN, NOT CAST."), then name and ₹price in the lower third, then at most one offer line.
  - There are no badges, annotations, grids or infographics.
- **Branding:** a small serif NAIRA wordmark, centred top or with the price; serif capitals with an italic secondary line; cream or ink type.
- **Copy:** a one-line sensory hook ("Anaar, pre-peeled.", "Looks like a proposal.", "Woven, not cast."), then the product name and ₹price, then one material fact, then the offer, free insured shipping and COD.
  - The headline is always "Product · ₹price" (catalogue: {{product.name}}). CTA is always Shop Now to the product page or /jewellery.
- **Offer:** every winner carries a bundle offer (BUY2 10% / BUY3 20%) or NAIRA10. The highest-ROAS catalogue ad (Core, 3.00x) used the single NAIRA10 code plus "Every piece here is in stock". The two winning videos used a struck "was" price.
- **Placement:** IG Feed 4:5 carries most of the purchases. Reels is cheapest per sale, but almost only with video. Story statics win only with the no-text ruby plate.

== 5. WHAT OUR LOSERS SHARE ==
- **Concept outranks product:** props, jokes or infographics dominate, and the piece is small or secondary (gummy bowl with doodles, lanterns, ruler, nutrition label, 2×2 grid).
- **Text-heavy layouts:** badges, category pills, highlighter marks, nutrition panels, multi-product grids, multiple prices. These earn clicks (some "play" ads had the top outbound CTR) but not sales.
- **Claim-led headlines** ("WATERPROOF.", "Tarnish 0%") lost on statics, including the like-for-like A/B in W1.
- **Pale pastel or silver-tone pieces on pale grounds** (Prism on powder stripes, Heartbead on sage) read low-contrast in Stories.
- **Formats and CTAs:** hand-built cover and lookbook carousels (0.35x); headline "Naira" instead of "Product · ₹price"; Learn More CTA.
- **Spend spread too thin:** 169 statics in 30 days averaged about ₹570 each, so most never got enough delivery to find out.
- **Over-tested products and repeats:** Prism Rivière and Woven Gold Hoops got about ₹42.6k of non-catalogue spend for 17 sales. Repeating the "rhyme" format did not repeat the win: the E2 fruit-plates campaign's 24 sales all came from ruby anaar; its other 20 plates made 0 on about ₹4.6k.
