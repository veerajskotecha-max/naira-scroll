== GLOBAL RULES AND FLAGS (apply to every card) ==
G1. The listings contradict the house rules.
  - Tags: 16 of the 18 carry "Hypoallergenic", "Tarnish Free" and "Waterproof". Only pearl-double-halo-studs and monochrome-enamel-hoops do not.
  - Plating lines: 15 say "anti-tarnish".
  - Care text: 15 say "Waterproof and tarnish free, so daily wear and water are fine". Toggle Link Chain was cleaned on 13 Nov; Pearl Double Halo and Monochrome Enamel never had it.
  - Ads must never quote these lines.
  - OWNER DECISION: the winners board suggests waterproof and tarnish-free claims. The store already makes them, but they are unsubstantiated and the house rule bans them. A claim would strengthen the "everyday, shower, gym" winner pattern a lot, but only with a real wear or water test on file. Until then, the closest lawful proxy is the listing's own PVD line: "PVD is bonded to the metal in a vacuum, so it is far harder [than regular plating]... keeps its colour much longer." Use that line only on the 16 SKUs whose listing says PVD.
G2. Banned everywhere: real, diamond, waterproof, tarnish-free, anti-tarnish, non tarnish, freshwater, hypoallergenic.
  - Never say "gold" or "silver" alone, and never "solid gold". Say gold tone, silver tone or rose gold tone.
  - Stone colour names are adjectives only: "emerald-green cubic zirconia", never "emerald". Ruby and amethyst work the same way.
  - "PVD" and "surgical stainless steel" are allowed only where that listing states them. All except Pearl Double Halo and Monochrome Enamel do.
G3. Stale sources. Do not take numbers from these:
  - The fidelity library (dated 19 Aug) has stale prices and stock for all 18. Brushed Huggies 12mm is now 14 x 9.8mm. Verdant Circlet 20mm is now 16.8mm. Toggle "zero stones, 15mm clasp" is wrong.
  - products_g1/g2.json also have stale prices: toggle 1299, woven 1299, brushed 999, ribbon 799, cushion 1199, eternity 1099, halo curve 1299. The toggle lock there gives "ring 15mm, bar 9mm"; the size check puts the ring at about 22mm and the bar at about 13-15mm.
  - Every finished ad in the repo bakes in old prices (for example "Triple Coil ₹1,499/₹1,899", "Marquise Choker ₹1,599"). Reuse only the text-free plates.
G4. Stock edge. Only 15 SKUs truly have 20+ units; the other three sit at exactly 20 and drop below with one sale:
  - At exactly 20: serpentine-whisper-chain, droplet-open-ring, bold-nocturne-drops.
  - No single variant reaches 20 on four multi-variant SKUs, so any colour- or metal-specific ad promotes a variant under 20:
    - Baguette Huggies: Ruby 0 (sold out), Crystal 10, Emerald 6, Amethyst 12.
    - Droplet Open Ring: Gold 9, Silver 11.
    - Marquise Drop Choker: Gold 15, Silver 9.
    - Triple Coil Wrap Bangle: Gold 20, Silver 19.
  - Never feature Ruby Baguette Huggies.
G5. Offers: NAIRA10, BUY2, BUY3, BUY4, free insured shipping and COD, as given. There is no verified review, rating or customer count in any source I read, so use none.
G6. Worn shots: every worn plate listed below was generated directly, not composited.
  - Plates marked "reads large" in /home/user/naira-scroll/docs/creative/performance/REDO43.md are excluded for scale honesty: colour/16, 36, 37, 44, 54 and 56.
  - The colour-camp plates and the performance plates passed construction checks against the listing photos. Their sizes were checked against the August copy, which matters for Brushed Huggies.
