## 1. Batch idea

**Make the piece wanted, and keep it the first thing you see.** b03 proved the monument method (stop 7.69, truth 8.19, 7 of 7
constructions held) but every frame was a bare product on a seamless, and desire fell to 6.94. b04 keeps what worked (the
reference's own pose, "exactly as in the reference", a centred subject, the sliding Feed crop, the extent gate first) and gives
every frame **one second element that makes the piece wanted**:

- **skin** (b04-1: the Toggle worn, deep warm skin, plum silk with a zari edge);
- **a literal rhyme** (b04-2: the Prism's three shades poured out as powder; b04-5: paperclips for paperclip links; b04-7: a
  marigold for the braid);
- **a drained world** (b04-3: a black-and-white wedding still life where only the Verdant Circlet keeps its colour);
- **a material and an evening light** (b04-4: one warm spot on teal velvet; b04-6: a warm rake on navy satin).

Every first beat is a reason to want the piece, taken from the listing ("Turn the clasp to the front", "One bold ear is enough",
"One clear point of focus", "Two hearts, read as a bow", "Texture does the talking"); the occasion goes in the second beat or the
picture. Two of the seven carry an honest surprise the model has already drawn or the layout controls (selective colour, b04-3;
the spotlit single point, b04-4), on top of the b04-0 float.
