creative director; B, product truth, compliance and performance), the fixes made after them, the re-scores of the older live
ads, and the shooting log.

- The fixer then changed three JPGs: the b04-1 Feed, the b03-1 Feed and the b03-1 Story.

- No ad file was changed after the final scores. The `review` blocks inside `b04-1.copy.json` and `b03-1.copy.json` were
  written before round 2. This review and `build/ledger.json` carry the round 2 scores.

## Prompting learnings

1. **Worn chain scale is set by counts and widths against body landmarks, not by a fraction of the body.**
   - "The ring is one fifth of the neck" came out as a quarter (0.248), with the chain hanging short.
   - Counts and widths fixed it to 0.215, with 13 links a side and a 2.7-ring-width drop:
     - "about five ring-widths wide, never four";
     - "about twelve paperclip links a side, never eight";
     - "the clasp about three ring-widths below the hollow of the throat".
2. **Front-on at 9:16, no crop wording keeps the chin out.** "The top edge just below the jaw" and "a crop that starts at the
   middle of the neck: the jaw and everything above it are outside the picture" were both ignored. A face-free worn Story needs
   either a camera a little above, looking down onto the collarbones, or a flat top band in the ground's colour.
3. **The safety filter refuses a headless crop worded mostly around chest and bare skin** (uncharged). The same prompt passed
   with two additions:
   - "a close crop of one person's neck and collarbones only";
   - "a jewellery close-up of the necklace for its listing, and they are dressed in a deep plum raw-silk blouse".

   Add jewellery and clothing words, not more body words.
4. **"Posed exactly as in reference 1 … copy its construction and pose, never its framing or its size" reproduced the Toggle
   clasp** (bar ÷ ring 0.64–0.67, one row of stones). With b03, construction has now held on 14 of 14 pictures.
5. **Plinth geometry must be locked in plain words.** "Front-on" plus "the front edge runs straight across" still gave a cube
   seen corner-on. This sentence fixed it on the first try for both b04-3 and b04-4: "a wide low slab: its front face is a flat
   band parallel to the frame's bottom edge that runs out past both side edges of the frame, its back edge is a level line hidden
   behind the piece, with no corners or side faces in view".
6. **Tie a prop's path, and a chain's path, to the piece's own units.**
   - Powder told to "run in from the left edge" rose to the back and wrapped the loop. "Level, within the loop's height, margins
     counted in stones" fixed it, though "stops one stone's width short" was still read as "ends at the loop".
   - A chain "along the front edge, seen from a little above" drew a deep U, 873 Feed px tall. These clauses gave a 2.1-thickness
     rise with no turn-back:
     - "rises by no more than about two chain thicknesses";
     - "camera level with the edge";
     - "ends running straight out at the bail's height".
7. **Margins asked in the piece's own widths come back at about 0.5–0.7× the ask:** one stud gave half a stud, and two pendant
   widths gave 1.42. A reference that fills its frame overrides any share given in words (72% against "a third"). Ask for about
   1.5 times the margin you want.
8. **A share below the band's floor is taken literally on a chain piece:** "one fifth of the frame's width" gave 18.2%. Never ask
   for less than the band's floor.
9. **The face must be tied to the frame edge.** "Unbroken face … no step or ledge" still drew a lower ledge 1550 px down the
   Story. Say "the face fills the frame to its bottom edge".
10. **Selective colour holds with zero leak, but the drained world must be recognisable without colour.** Black-and-white
    "marigold petals" render as generic white petals, so the frame reads as a naturally monochrome set. Name a prop whose shape
    says what it is, or carry the device in the type (the keyword in the piece's colour, board 85).
11. **Directed light does not render on `nano_banana_2`.** These all came out soft:
    - "one small warm spot finds the stone" gave a broad flat top light, twice;
    - the b03 pool had a soft edge;
    - L2 flash rendered as a soft key.

    "Lights aimed at the piece so the backdrop falls off" darkens the ground (L\* 35 to 23.6) but draws no spot. Brief surprises
    the layout or the object makes, not ones the light makes.
12. **Measure before calling a shadow absent or an extent clear.**
    - On a near-black face, a contact shadow renders at ΔL\* 3–5. Profile the rows under the piece against the same rows
      elsewhere.
    - Count out-of-focus edge stubs and the shadow core in the strict 380–930 window. When a strict window exists (b04-4: P
      33.33–33.84), lay out inside it.
13. **Credits:** 2 per picture, and a refused job is not charged. b04 spent 14 of 14.46.
