## Fix target for the next batch

**Desire** (both critics: A 7.13, B 6.75; mean 6.94, the lowest dimension). In b04, every frame carries one second element that
makes the piece wanted, while the piece still lands first at 200 px.

1. **At least one worn frame with real skin,** on a hanging piece with 20+ units. Use one of the two proven no-face geometries:
   b02-1's camera directly behind the head, or F2's lips-to-chest crop (no eyes).
   - Deep warm skin with visible pores.
   - One wedding-season cue in the picture, such as a zari or silk neckline edge.
   - A dark saturated ground.
   - The key detail at 15% or more of the Feed width.
   - Scale locked by a body ratio first (for example, "the chain runs along the collarbone, about one third of the shoulder
     width"), measured, and dropped above 1.2×.
2. **Every product-only frame carries a world.** Use one of two routes:
   - **An A3 Literal Rhyme:** one in-season prop that rhymes in colour or material and doubles as the true-scale referent
     (rule 10). Examples:
     - a mogra bud of about 2 cm beside the Pearl studs on ink;
     - a marigold head beside the Brushed huggies on plum;
     - cutting chai and a mishri crystal beside Halo Curve's two stones.
   - **A time of day in the light:** an evening pool or a warm window rake on a material surface (glaze, silk, plaster).

   Either way:
   - Dark saturated grounds only: plum, ink, oxblood, deep teal, or "dark sage, as dark as forest moss".
   - No mid-tone cocoa or sage.
   - A matte floor where the card sits.
3. **Stories put the piece on a riser or plinth whose front face fills the lower third,** so the band under 1250 holds material,
   not dead floor.
4. **The first beat carries a reason to want the piece** (a benefit or a device), and the occasion goes in the second beat
   (sangeet, mehendi, shaadi guest).

- On the blind 200 px sheet, the piece lands first and the rhyme or skin second.

## Prompting learnings

1. **Size words do not shrink a lone product.**
   - With the 0.7× rule, renders still came out at 1.3–1.85× the share asked.
   - b03-4 try 2 asked for about a third of try 1's share ("one twelfth of the height, a quarter of the width, the group one
     sixth of the height"). It rendered 2.5–2.65× the ask, larger than try 1.
   - `nano_banana_2`, served every time, has a preferred hero size for a lone product: about 47–76% of the frame width and 21–26%
     of the height. Size phrases below it are ignored.
   - Brief layouts for a subject extent of about 700 Feed px, or go card-only. When the extent band is tight, ask for 0.5× the
     share wanted, not 0.7×.
2. **Pose and aspect move the extent; size words do not.** b03-3 went from 26% to 21% of the height, with the width unchanged at
   47%, by copying the reference's own pose ("the loop reaches out to the LEFT past the tiger eye … about 1.3 times as wide as
   tall; never stood up tall") and raising the camera to 35°.
3. **Shadow geometry can be described, and it renders.** "Short, tucked directly behind and under, no further than a quarter of
   the ring's height; never a long shadow sideways" removed a frame-wide shadow.
4. **A floating pair cannot fit the headline-plus-card gate (550 px) at the model's hero size.**
   - Hoop (about 21% of the height) + gap (at least 0.4×) + shadow (about 0.3×) comes to about 700 Feed px.
   - Brief floats as card-only (gate 84–930) with a budget of about 720 px, or float a single piece.
   - Overhead L6 gives physically right parallelogram footprints, not C shapes; do not require "the same C shape".
5. **Locks and reference poses held on all 7 pictures, with no morph.** "Exactly as in the macro/pack reference" reproduced:
   - Cushion: 4 claws and a single-row cushion halo;
   - Brushed: the satin finish;
   - Monochrome: 1 + 1 stripes, 3 rails and an open C;
   - Pearl: two bands with a bridged gap;
   - Halo Curve: two unmatched stones in an open bypass.
6. **Light.**
   - "L2 direct on-camera flash" rendered as a soft key twice: the model does not do hard frontal flash.
   - L6, L7 and the L1 pool rendered, but the pool edge is soft, never crisp.
   - Log the light that rendered.
7. **Scene words that failed.**
   - "No horizon line" was ignored at a 35° camera: a seam appeared.
   - "A satin-sheen glaze floor, no mirror reflection" gave a diffuse reflection glow under the card. Ask for a matte floor
     where the card sits.
8. **Colour words leak.**
   - "A soft pink-white lustre" gave a blush pearl (a* about 7 against the ref's 2–4).
   - The next Pearl prompt should say:
     - "a white pearl with a faint warm cream lustre, never pink";
     - "a neutral white key on the piece";
     - "about five tiny bridges, unevenly spaced";
     - "each band one row of distinct round stones about 1 mm, never frosted or glitter".
   - Never write "pink".
9. **Measure acceptance ratios from the ref photo at the same pose.** Monochrome H ÷ W is 1.37 on the pack ref, not the brief's
   1.65. Check listing sizes against the refs too: Halo Curve's 11.8 mm is probably the stone's length, not its face.
10. **"Deep sage" rendered mid-tone** (L 113–130 on the floor), so 26 px bone was only about 4:1. Ask for "dark sage, as dark as
    forest moss", or sample a scrim from the wall.
    - **Finisher note:** wall-sampled scrims behind 26 px callouts need a wide feather (40 px inside the box) at about .5.
      Narrow 24 px feathers read as tiles on a flat ground.
