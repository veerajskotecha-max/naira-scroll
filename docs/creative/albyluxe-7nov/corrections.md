## How this audit was done

- **Reviewers.** Six reviewers each took three pieces (L15–L18 four). They were given only the supplier's and your photos (`refs/`), the listing photos (`build/out/`), the copy, the specs, the size cards and the palm scales, and not my earlier conclusions. Each read every frame of every colour at full resolution, with zoomed crops where a stone, claw or link mattered.
- **What they measured:** sizes against the body (finger 16-18 mm, earlobe about 18 mm, fingernail 10-12 mm, neck about 11 cm), stone colour in CIELab against the supplier's, and pixel spans for every proportion.
- **My checks.** I re-checked every must-fix item, and several should-fix ones, side by side with the supplier photo before writing it down. A few claims changed on that check: the L12 size, after the L08 dimension lines were re-read, and the L14 zigzag, after a close look at the supplier's baskets. I also scanned all 122 gallery frames for the old-print border (which found L08_W1), checked that the exports, page photos and cards match the current frames, and checked the copy for banned words and internal notes.
- **One reversal of my own earlier work:** the L04 flag said the owner's upper-right stone is smaller. Measured at the claws, it is not.
- **Your note on the page.** L01 "change" (remove pic 3, keep the rest) was read from the approval page and is followed here. No other piece had a decision, and there is no sign-off.
- **Working crops** are in the session's scratch folder and are not committed. Nothing in `build/out/`, `exports/`, `cards/`, the page or Shopify was changed by this audit.
