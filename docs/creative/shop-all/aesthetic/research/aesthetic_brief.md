# Shop All, round 2: the aesthetic ten (7 Nov 2026)

Read BRIEF.md first (facts, bans, formats). Where BRIEF.md or BUILD.md and this file differ, this file wins.

## The ask
The founder saw the first ten Shop All ads (loud, lo-fi, clever: an exam paper, a gifting list, playing cards, a masala
dabba, a price-sum, a text post) and wants ten MORE AESTHETIC ones: "Make more aesthetic shop all ads using existing high
CTR creatives. Be creative and make 10." Same job (send people to ALL of Naira's jewellery, https://nairaflore.com/jewellery),
same pool of existing approved photos (pool/pool.json; no new photos can be made), but these must look like the most
beautiful ad in her feed: the kind a stylish 28-year-old in Mumbai would screenshot. Beauty is the hook; a quiet, clever
line earns the click.

## What "aesthetic" means here (the bar every concept and build is judged on)
1. **One dominant image, lots of air.** One photo carries the frame, cropped with intent (rule of thirds, the jewel at a
   power point). If a frame shows several pieces, it is a disciplined composition (a diptych, a triptych, a 2x2 at most,
   equal sizes, generous even gutters), never a busy collage. Negative space is a feature: at least a third of the frame
   is quiet (paper, plaster, linen, or the photo's own calm area).
2. **Editorial type.** Display: Cormorant Garamond (CG / CGI italic) or Velista caps, set with care (tracking, optical
   size, line breaks that read as poetry). Small labels: Jost caps, tracked 0.18-0.3em, 22-30 px. Body copy only if the
   format needs it, 28-34 px CG. NO handwriting (Kalam, Caveat, Marker), no Anton, no Inter Bold memes, no stickers,
   no marker circles, arrows, ticks, tape, emoji, badges, bursts or price tags shouting.
3. **Palette from the photo.** Grounds: Naira cream/paper, ink, or a flat colour sampled from the photo itself
   (the blush of the plaster, the aqua of the rangoli, the maroon of the saree, the sage of the linen). At most one accent,
   sampled from the jewel (gold #9A6B2F / #C9A15B, ruby, emerald, aqua). Never pure white, never neon.
4. **Little text.** On-image text under about 12% of the frame. A headline of 2-6 words; one small breadth line; the offer
   only as a whisper (small caps, one line) or not at all. No price on more than three pieces in a frame.
5. **Graphic devices allowed** (use few, use well): hairline rules (1-2 px ink or gold), a passe-partout / mat-board frame
   around a photo, a gallery wall label (piece name, "gold tone, cubic zirconia", price) set like a museum card, folio
   numbers and running heads like a printed book, colour swatches sampled from the photo (named after Indian things:
   anaar red, elaichi green, haldi yellow, rangoli aqua, mishri white, kesar gold), a thin gold keyline, a soft natural
   shadow under a framed print, a subtle paper grain. Every device must look intentional and be aligned to a grid.
6. **Finish.** Margins at least 72 px, everything on a grid, optical alignment, no widows, no text touching jewellery,
   no cut-off letters, nothing accidental. The jewellery is sharp, correctly coloured, never upscaled past 1.0x, never
   recoloured, warped or repainted. One NAIRA logo (logo_ink on light, logo_white on dark), 200-300 px wide.

## How it must still sell the WHOLE shop (not one product)
Each idea has to open the door to everything: by naming the collection's breadth (rings, earrings, necklaces, bracelets;
₹599 to ₹2,399), by showing a curated edit (2-4 pieces as a set: "the colour edit", "the gold edit"), or by framing the
one hero as "one of the collection" (an index, a contents page, a gallery with more rooms, a colour chart with more
colours). The line under the hero should make someone curious about what else is in there.
Range wording: 133 pieces were in stock on 7 Nov, so "130+ pieces" is true but tight. Prefer count-free wording:
"from ₹599", "₹599 to ₹2,399", "the whole collection", "every piece". If a count is used it must be "130+".

## Built on what clicked (use these photos and lessons)
- H01 "Almost edible." Prism Rivière in sugared sweets: 6.21% CTR Story, the best-selling concept ad.
- H02 Prism Rivière on three stripes of rangoli: 6.10% CTR Story ("Rangoli, in a line.").
- H07 / H08 Woven Gold Hoops: 5.81-6.08% CTR ("Woven, not cast.").
- H06 ruby necklace with anaar: the successor of "Anaar, pre-peeled." (5.49%, 15 sales at 3.63x).
- H18 ruby bracelet with anaar (E2 fruit plate, no text): 4.32% CTR, 24 purchases. SOLD OUT: see its rule in pool.json.
- H04 Prism Cluster Studs with rangoli cones: 5.21% Feed. H05 mesh cuff and pheni 4.32%. H09 huggies and potlis 4.01%.
- The on-model listing photos (worn/): the Bestsellers catalogue carousel built on them runs 5.73% CTR with 43
  purchases at 2.67x in the last 14 days: the account's best purchase driver. They are beautiful when cropped tight
  (ear, collarbone, wrist) against their warm beige ground.
- Lessons: one piece, one ground, lots of air; Stories beat Feeds; the best seller had NO words on the picture (the
  picture sells, the line earns the stop); a familiar Indian thing turned into a line works ("Rangoli, in a line.",
  "Almost edible."); "X, not Y" craft truths work; generic occasion lines ("Plans tonight?") fail.

## Do not repeat the first ten
They are in check/final10_0.jpg and check/final10_1.jpg. Do not reuse their formats (text post, exam question, list,
playing cards, spice tin, price sum, five-tile shringar grid, filter pills, saree plus tiles) or their headlines.
Reusing a pool photo is fine if the treatment is new.

## Ideas the founder might love (seeds, not a menu; better ideas welcome)
A gallery wall with museum labels and a red "sold" dot on the anaar bracelet ("Sold. The rest are on view.");
a contents page of a printed book where chapters are categories and folio numbers are prices; a colour chart sampled
from the stones; a diptych that rhymes a jewel with an Indian thing in the same colour; a single extreme crop of a stone
with a one-line poem; a passe-partout framed print; a book spread with a running head; a still life titled like a
painting; a lookbook index; a monochrome edit (all gold tone, all green); "Collected works".

No imitation of real brands, magazines or documents (no Vogue-style mastheads other than NAIRA's own logo, no real
museum names). No fake reviews, counts or urgency.
