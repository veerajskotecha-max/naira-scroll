# Caption rules for the Shop All ads

- 1. Length and shape: one paragraph, 120-180 characters, no line breaks. The hook and the range fact must sit inside the first 125 characters so they show before '...more'. Model: 'Rangoli, in a line. One of 130+ pieces from ₹599: the Prism Rivière Bracelet, silver tone, ₹2,399. Buy 2, get 10% off. Buy 3, get 20% off, applied in your bag.' (159 characters)
- 2. First 2-5 words: name one familiar Indian thing that is really in the photo (rangoli, anaar, pheni, mishri, elaichi, potli, toran, a saree pallu) and turn it, or state a craft truth as 'X, not Y' ('Woven, not cast.'). Never open with a percentage, a price, the brand name, 'Shop', 'Celebrate' or 'Discover', or an occasion that could sell anything ('Date night.', 'Dress code: sparkle.').
- 3. Sell the range in the next clause: '130+ pieces from ₹599' (never 135, never a rounded-up boast). Add the four categories when there is room: rings, earrings, necklaces, bracelets. For a pool hero you may say 'our most-clicked pieces', and nowhere else.
- 4. Name the piece shown, with its exact pool.json price (₹ sign, Indian comma: ₹1,199) and one exact detail (25 mm, ruby red, adjustable US 5 to 9). The photo earns the stop; the caption proves the stop was worth it.
- 5. The offer goes last, in these words: 'Buy 2, get 10% off. Buy 3, get 20% off, applied in your bag.' No codes (drop BUY2/BUY3), no 'sale', no percent-off against compare-at prices, and never the offer in the first five words.
- 6. Add one proof line, rotated across the 10 ads: 'Free insured shipping, 3-5 working days.' / '7-day returns.' / 'Cash on delivery on serviceable pincodes.' (never 'across India') / 'Every piece arrives in the Naira gift box.' At most two per caption. No ratings, order counts or reviews.
- 7. Material words: 'gold tone' and 'silver tone' only (drop '18k' and 'rhodium'); 'cubic zirconia' or the colour (ruby red, emerald green, aqua); 'shell pearl'; no gem names (tiger's eye, emerald or ruby as nouns). Never: real, diamond, gold, silver, waterproof, tarnish words, hypoallergenic, stunning, elevate, must-have, luxury.
- 8. Formatting: sentence case, full stops, no emoji, no exclamation marks, no hashtags, no pipes, no ALL CAPS except one short word at most. The quiet caption is the differentiator in a feed of 🪔 and 'FLAT 50% OFF'.
- 9. Hinglish dose: at most one Hindi word or short phrase, used as or inside the hook, that an urban reader needs no gloss for (kabhi, potli, choodi, ladi, ghungroo). Everything else in plain English.
- 10. No urgency or scarcity of any kind: no countdowns, 'limited time', 'only X left', 'hurry' or festival deadlines, even where stock is genuinely low (the Ruby huggies have 4 units).
- 11. CTA and headline: leave 'Shop now' to Meta's button and keep it out of the text. Headline at most 40 characters, carrying the range and the entry price ('130+ pieces, from ₹599' or 'All pieces · from ₹599'). Link to https://nairaflore.com/jewellery.
- 12. The transfer test: if the caption would still work under a different photo or another brand's ad, rewrite it. Never name or allude to a competitor, and never compare.
