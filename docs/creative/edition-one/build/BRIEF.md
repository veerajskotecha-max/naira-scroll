## FAIL for any of these
1. **Product misrepresented:**
   - a countable fact is wrong (stone count, order or colour, pearl count, number of petals);
   - the construction, shape or metal is wrong (gold vs white);
   - parts are missing or added;
   - detail is visibly melted or garbled at phone size.
   If a feature is too small to resolve, say so and judge only what can be seen.
2. **Wrong pieces:** an extra piece of jewellery or a third earring. Frames may show one or both of the pair.
3. **Worn and hand frames:**
   - ANY eye, eyelash, eyebrow, nose, lips, mouth, chin or jawline visible, even at the edge, is a fail.
   - Hands must have five natural fingers and correct anatomy.
   - The piece must sit where it really would.
4. **Scale more than about 30% off**, judged against the stated referent or the body. Referents:
   | Referent | Size |
   |---|---|
   | fingernail | ~10mm wide |
   | adult ear | ~60mm tall |
   | ring-shaped mint | ~15mm |
   | pea | ~8mm |
   | barfi square | ~30mm |
   | gift box | ~50mm |
   | porcelain bell plate | ~40mm |
   | espresso cup | ~60mm tall |
   | star anise | ~30mm |
   | jaali opening | ~60mm |
   | shisha mirror | ~12mm |
   | mishri crystal | ~10mm |
   | mini croissant | ~80mm |
   | cardamom pod | ~15mm |
   | ice-cube tray cell | ~30mm |
   | specimen box | ~150mm wide |
   | gerbera | ~90mm |
   | pointe-shoe toe | ~60mm |
   | nail-paint bottle | ~50mm tall |
   | tap spout mouth | ~15mm |
   | sherbet glass | ~70mm wide |

   A soft referent with no fixed size (a powder band, a fabric fold, light) is not a scale fail.
5. **Physically impossible:** floating, no contact, or a closed hinge drawn open.
6. **Text, letters, numbers, logos or watermarks** anywhere, including on packets, bottles, plates or paper. A shape made by
   the jewellery itself (such as a question mark) is fine.
7. **Off-brand:** a white, grey or black backdrop, a cool or green cast over the whole frame, a beige, sand or tan backdrop,
   or clutter. Small coloured props (a green pod, a croissant) are fine.

## NOT a fail (notes only)
Composition, camera angle, depth of field, prop arrangement, environment tint on metal that still reads as the right
metal, and minor background texture.

## Score (0–10) as an ad photograph, only if it passes
Rate it as a senior art director for a witty, slow-luxury demi-fine brand: blush, ivory, dusty rose, soft Indian afternoon
light. 10 = campaign hero; 8.5 = runs as-is; below 8.5 = do not run. The camps are meant to be CRAZY: the idea must read in
one second (the piece replaces or rhymes with an everyday object). Weight the idea most, then:
- the product as the brightest, clearest thing;
- light (soft, plus a hard specular);
- realism (texture, grain, contact);
- calm copy space;
- thumb-stopping quality.
