# Colour Camps — APRICOT, SAGE, LILAC

Fifteen in-stock Naira Petite pieces, photographed inside three colour worlds, checked frame by frame against their
live listings, and built into paid-social ads and three exhibition standee designs. Built 27 Sep 2026.

Artifact (every ad, carousel and print file, with save buttons): https://claude.ai/artifact/16NXisPUtfeiZFjV5XNNgy

## What is in this folder

| Path | Holds |
|---|---|
| `ads/<CAMP>/` | One hero ad per product in two formats: story 1080×1920 and feed 1080×1350 (JPG) |
| `carousels/<CAMP>/` | Carousel cards 2…n (1080×1350). Card 1 of every carousel is that product's feed hero in `ads/` |
| `standees/preview/` | JPG previews of the three roll-up designs at four sizes |
| (artifact) | The twelve print PDFs at exact roll-up size, 7–13 MB each. They live in the artifact rather than in git; `build/standee/` regenerates them |

| `build/` | The code and records: shoot prompts, grading and retouch tools, the verification brief and every verdict, the ad system, the standee generator, the export and the artifact page |

## How the photographs were made

Higgsfield, Nano Banana Pro at 2K, with the product's own listing photo as the only reference and one product per
frame, using the six-block prompt from the Naira image skill (shot, fidelity lock, scale, scene, light, negatives).
Four frames per product: S1 hero (9:16), S2 worn (4:5), S3 macro (4:5), S4 flat lay (4:5). Prompts are generated by
`build/shoot/ladder.py`; every job id is in `build/shoot/keymap.json`.

## How they were checked

Every frame went to an independent reviewer with the listing facts and every listing photo
(`build/verify/BRIEF.md`). A frame failed for a wrong count, changed construction or clasp, wrong metal or finish, any
eye, lip, nose or chin in a worn shot, a piece reading more than about 30% off its listed size against a known object
or the body, impossible physics, text, or a frame that does not read as its camp colour. Verdicts are in
`build/verify/*.json`. Nothing unverified reaches an ad: `build/shoot/mkfinal.py` picks only passed frames.

What failed, and what was done about it:

- **Earrings next to fruit read oversized.** The huggies and bows looked 1.6–2× their size beside an apricot half or an
  almond. They were reshot with no size prop at all, and the silk weave sets the scale (huggies S1 and S4, bows S4).
  The reshots passed.
- **Toggle Link Chain clasp.** The real closure is a spring-gate sailor ring (gate, grooved collar, push-pin). Three
  frames drew a plain closed ring. The hero was repaired with an image edit that used the clasp from the listing's
  on-body photo as its reference, and it passed. The macro and flat lay were dropped.
- **Chevron Whisper Ring.** The sage leaf was drawn too small next to it, so the ring read 1.5× its size. The hero
  was reshot without a prop and passed. A second reshot with a larger leaf still failed, and the flat lay was dropped.
- **Worn earrings.** Three worn frames (Woven Gold Hoops, Brushed Gold Huggies, Pearl Blossom) still showed a chin,
  an eye or lashes at the edge after cropping, and were dropped rather than risk a face in a jewellery ad. The worn
  Ribbon Bow frame read about 40% oversized against the ear and was dropped too.
- **Heartline Paperclip flat lay.** The hearts read about 1.7× their size next to the apricot and the frame was
  dropped. The hero took six attempts before the nine haloed heart stations were drawn correctly.
- **Ribbon Bead Bracelet.** The bow read about 1.5× its size worn and in the flat lay. Both were dropped, since the
  piece has three units in stock.
- **Granule Dome Ring flat lay.** The dome was drawn as a separate loose part, so the frame was dropped.

## Edits after the shoot

These edits change backgrounds and framing, not the pieces:

- **Sage grade** (`grade.py`): only low-saturation green pixels are pulled toward the brand sage. On the heroes the
  piece is also masked out with its cut-out; on the other sage frames the reviewers checked the metal for any shift
  (one macro keeps a negligible mint edge on a highlight). Files with a `g` suffix.
- **Crops** (`recrop.py`): worn frames are cropped to remove face edges, keeping at least 1080 px of width. Files with
  a `c` suffix.
- **Trio Oval Drop hero** (`retouch_corner.py`): the cream table showing past the torn paper in the top corners was
  recoloured lilac, with the earrings masked out (zero weight inside the cut-out). File suffix `r`.
- **Standee heroes** were upscaled to 2294×4096 with the Higgsfield upscaler and cut out with its background remover.
  The upscaled Heartbead plate was graded exactly like the verified one.

## The standees

Three roll-up designs, each built from one camp's photographs and its device, at four sizes:
61 × 152.4 cm (2 × 5 ft), 76.2 × 182.6 cm (2.5 × 6 ft), 91.4 × 182.6 cm (3 × 6 ft) and 122 × 182.6 cm (4 × 6 ft).

- **A · APRICOT, the fruit stall.** The word is split around the hoops: APRI above, COT. below, both set in the calm
  silk the photograph leaves for them. A giant PLU sticker carries the price, the other four pieces rise out of crate
  photographs with their own stickers, and a taped strip carries the three facts.
- **B · SAGE, the colour card.** The stand is a paint sample card. The top swatch is the photograph, with SAGE. behind
  the Heartbead bracelet as it climbs over the swatch edge. A paint strip runs lightest to deepest sage with one piece
  per chip and its real hex code, and the deepest chip is the QR code.
- **C · LILAC, the stamp sheet.** A philatelic souvenir sheet. A giant perforated stamp holds the Prism Rivière with
  LILAC. behind it, the bracelet climbs out over the perforations, and a postmark cancels the corner. The rest of the
  issue follows as a block of stamps, with the price as the denomination, and an airmail border runs along the foot.

Print notes: each PDF is set at the exact size, with photographs and colours running to every edge. The bottom 12 cm
is a plain band because it sits in the stand's base, and the top 3 cm stays clear for the rail. The hero photographs
print at roughly 65–105 dpi at full size, from upscaled 4K sources, which suits viewing from a metre or more. Other
images are capped at 1.5× their printed pixels, and shadows are baked into the cut-outs so nothing is rasterised
twice. Files are RGB; the print shop converts. The QR codes open `nairaflore.com` with `utm_source=standee&utm_medium=print` and
`utm_campaign` set to the camp.

## Running it

Stock and prices were re-read from Shopify on 27 Sep 2026, just before export. All fifteen pieces are active, and
prices match the ads. Woven Gold Hoops 77, Toggle Link Chain 80, Prism Rivière 77, Heartbead Bracelet 29, Brushed
Gold Huggies 16; the other ten have 3–5 units each.

- Always on: Woven Gold Hoops, Toggle Link Chain, Prism Rivière.
- Secondary: Heartbead Bracelet, Brushed Gold Huggies.
- Pieces with 3–5 units should run in carousels or with capped budgets. Pause any ad at 2 units.

## Claims

Only what the listings state: waterproof, surgical stainless steel, 18K gold tone or rhodium plated, with sizes and
stone details from each listing. Recheck prices before launching if they change after 27 Sep.

## Rebuilding

`build/ads/run.py heroes_all render` and `build/ads/run.py car_all render "" feed` render the ads,
`build/standee/{apricot,sage,lilac}.py` render the standees, `build/ads/export_cc.py` exports everything, and
`build/artifact/build_page.py` builds the artifact page. Paths inside the scripts point at the build machine's
scratch folders; the plates they read are the ones in `plates/`.
