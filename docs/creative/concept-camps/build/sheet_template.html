        How these were made and checked

        Every frame was made on Higgsfield from the piece’s own listing photos, one piece per frame. The image model was Nano Banana Pro at 2K; the candy test used Nano Banana 2 Lite at 1K. Each prompt has six parts: the shot, a lock on what must not change, true scale against a known object, the scene, two lights, and what must never appear.

        An independent reviewer then checked every frame against the listing photos for construction, stone count and order, metal, one piece only, no face, true scale and the blush palette. A frame that passes is scored as an ad out of 10, and only 8.5 or more runs. Crops were cut only where the one fault sat at an edge, and were checked again.
