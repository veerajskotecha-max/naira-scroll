# Research corpus

Four prior research artifacts, merged into machine-readable references so the creative work has one
place to check a number instead of nine. Compiled 19 Aug 2026 by parallel agents, each reading its
sources in full rather than summarising from memory.

| File | Sources merged | Size |
|---|---|---|
| `01-competitor-corpus.md` | Six Ways to Sell Jewellery (498 creatives, 6 brands) · Palmona creative brief · Demi-Fine Competitive Dossier (22 India + 10 global players) · The Swarovski Benchmark | 8,400 words |
| `02-production-craft.md` | Bambi Swim Ad Production Research · Ad Desk · Indian Demi-Fine Review Photo Field Spec · Lac & Bone Feed 01 | 12,800 words |
| `03-audit-history.md` | SEO Audit · Catalogue Fidelity Audit · Image Approval · Frame Review & Catalogue Conflicts · Source Verification | 10,200 words |

## What each is for

**`01-competitor-corpus.md`** — the evidence behind the creative position. Per-brand ad profiles
with creative counts and impression ranks, the vocabulary ownership matrix, offer mechanics as raw
counts, four price ladders, routing data, and **the struck list**: nine claims circulating in
competitor decks and agency blogs that do not survive checking. It also flags four cross-document
contradictions rather than silently resolving them — most importantly that one source says
"Palmona" and two say "Palmonas", which may be two different brands.

**`02-production-craft.md`** — how to build and judge an ad. Script beat structure, the corrected
KPI table (every original target was wrong and the file says why), the gated kill sequence, fatigue
signals, the demand calendar, and §8 on AI image generation: fidelity-lock language, scale
technique, the two-light rule, and the single-reference law that `../creative/two-slot-system.md`
tests and revises.

**`03-audit-history.md`** — 172 individually itemised catalogue defects plus 11 places where two
audits contradict each other about the same SKU. Ends in a reusable pre-flight checklist covering
image, copy, and Shopify data hygiene.

## Reading them honestly

Each file separates what its sources measured from what they asserted. Where a source gave no
figure, the merge records the gap rather than estimating — there is no India-specific demi-fine CPM
or CTR benchmark anywhere in this corpus, and none was invented to fill the hole. Claims the
sources themselves graded as unverified stay graded that way.
